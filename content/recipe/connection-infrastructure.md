# Connection Infrastructure

> Build recurring spaces and rhythms (same time, same place, food, no agenda) that make connection inevitable.

*The need:* Isolation and loneliness.

There are real dangers of isolation and loneliness. Instead of one-off events or projects, this recipe builds actual infrastructure (spaces, rhythms, and practices) that makes connection inevitable rather than impossible: the same time, the same place, with no ulterior motive beyond togetherness, and that intention made explicit. Consistency matters more than perfection, food transforms everything, different people lead different weeks, and the design works against cliques by keeping people mixing. Five people truly connecting beats fifty who are not.

**How it works**
1. Map when and where people are isolated.
2. Pick a time and place that works for those people.
3. Make it the same every single week or month; consistency matters.
4. Remove every barrier so the event is free, accessible, and welcoming.
5. Design for mixing, with activities that enable meaningful interaction.
6. Let it become self-organizing over time.

**Ingredients**
- a place people can linger
- a regular sacred time
- food
- someone good at introductions
- cooperative activities
- childcare and accessibility

**Minimum resources:** Free with potluck food and a consistent space.

**You'll know it's working when**
- people show up even when they don't 'need' to
- spontaneous mutual aid
- 'I don't know anyone' becomes 'I have friends here'

**Field tips**
- consistency over perfection
- food transforms everything
- design against cliques
- small is fine
