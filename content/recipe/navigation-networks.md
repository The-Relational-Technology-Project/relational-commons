# Navigation Networks

> Neighbors who've navigated complex systems literally walk others through them.

*The need:* Resources exist but systems are too complex to access.

Resources exist (food assistance, healthcare, housing help) but people cannot access them because the systems are too complex and the barriers too high. This recipe creates human bridges: instead of handing out phone numbers or websites, it builds networks of neighbors who have successfully navigated these systems and will literally walk others through the process, documenting every barrier and workaround and organizing to remove the systemic ones. Lived experience matters more than professional credentials, and the work is made celebratory rather than clinical.

**How it works**
1. Find people who have successfully navigated systems; they are your experts.
2. Pay them as navigators.
3. When someone needs help, a navigator physically goes with them.
4. Document every barrier and every workaround.
5. Share what works across the network.
6. When you find systemic barriers, organize to remove them, including publishing better guidance than currently exists.

**Ingredients**
- 5-10 people who've navigated systems
- a way to reach out (text/WhatsApp/office hours)
- transport money or drivers
- sympathetic insiders
- a shared tracking doc

**Minimum resources:** Navigator stipends unless volunteer.

**You'll know it's working when**
- unused resources get used
- agencies ask navigators how to simplify
- helped people become navigators

**Field tips**
- lived experience over credentials
- build insider relationships
- make it celebratory

**Building from (real projects)**
- Community Help Desk
- FYLPRO COVID-19 Task Force
- Up the Block
- Zinester's Guide
