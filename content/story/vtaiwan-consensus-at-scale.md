# vTaiwan: consensus at national scale

> Taiwan's vTaiwan process pairs Pol.is opinion mapping with face-to-face deliberation: crowdsourced statements reveal rough consensus, then stakeholders meet to work the remaining tensions. It shaped national policy, most famously the ride-sharing rules.

**Place:** Taiwan.

vTaiwan remains the reference case for scaling listening without flattening it. Statements are crowdsourced and voted on in Pol.is until the opinion groups and the bridging statements are visible; then the people who disagree meet in person to work the parts the map could not settle. The ride-sharing (Uber) rules are the best-known outcome.

**The pattern a neighborhood can borrow:** opinion map online first, then work the hard parts face to face. The map is the agenda, not the decision.
