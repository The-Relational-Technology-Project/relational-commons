# Food With Neighbors Fund (The Island, Austin, Chicago, IL)

> The Island Civic Association in Chicago's Austin community area funds residents to host 'simple, low-lift gatherings that bring neighbors together with food'. Fixed $100 paid by Venmo or PayPal, with a three-attendee minimum and a short post-event survey.

## The invitation
Headline: "Food With Neighbors" under the Island Civic Association masthead. Promise: "Give residents of The Island funds to host simple, low-lift gatherings that bring neighbors together with food." The button says **Apply Now**. It is for residents of The Island neighborhood only.

## The grant
Fixed **$100** per applicant, paid directly via Venmo or PayPal. Gatherings must happen by the end of October 2026; the application stays open while funds last. Asked of hosts: at least 3 attendees besides the host, a post-event survey, and an optional photo.

## What the landing page does well
- It lives inside an existing civic association site (nav with member and donation links, Nextdoor and email-list links in the footer), so the grant reads as something the neighborhood already does, not a new brand.
- One large hero image, one intro paragraph, one offer with a button: the whole pitch fits above the FAQ.
- A six-question FAQ covers exactly what a nervous first-time host asks: how to apply, deadline, how money arrives, what it can be spent on, how many people must come, what is owed afterward.
- A named human contact (Nate Tubbs) rather than a generic inbox.
- "Doesn't have to be complicated" is the recurring reassurance.
- Visual feel: civic-association branding with a black-and-white logo; the hero image is the only color note (not described further on the site).
