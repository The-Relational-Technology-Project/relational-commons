# Know Your Neighbors Fund (New Haven, CT)

> Civic Works' Know Your Neighbors Fund gives '$200 grants for projects that bring people together in New Haven. From morning chess clubs to block potlucks.' Fixed $200, open to any New Haven resident, with a preference for gatherings that recur.

## The invitation
Headline: "APPLY FOR THE Know Your Neighbors Fund." Promise (also the site's meta description): "$200 grants for neighbor-led projects that bring people together in New Haven. From morning chess clubs to block potlucks — small ideas that build community connection." The button says **Apply Today!** It is "Open to all New Haven residents". The short domain knowyourneighborsfund.org redirects to the Civic Works page.

## The grant
Fixed **$200**. Deadline September 30th. Rules: applicant must live in New Haven and the event must happen there; the event "must be free of charge and open to the public"; projects "should ideally be recurring activities". Afterward, "a simple report": answers to 3-4 questions, attendance count, 2-3 photos, and a brief story or quote. With Neighbors administers this round and may feature stories.

## What the landing page does well
- A memorable standalone domain (knowyourneighborsfund.org) that forwards to the organization's page, so the fund is shareable by name.
- The promise sentence carries two concrete examples (morning chess club, block potluck) that set the scale instantly.
- "Strong communities are built one neighbor at a time" states the belief in one line before the rules.
- It asks for recurring traditions, not one-off parties, and says so up front.
- The report is specified exactly (3-4 questions, headcount, 2-3 photos, a quote), which makes "what do I owe you?" a non-question.
- Visual feel: clean, modern nonprofit layout with community photography; colors not stated on the fetched page.
