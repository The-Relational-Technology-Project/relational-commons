# Neighbor Gathering Grants (Falls Church, VA)

> Falls Church Forward asks 'People in Falls Church are meeting their neighbors. Want in?' and offers microgrants from $50 to $150 for gatherings held August through October. A range grant whose landing page leads with a photo carousel of what past hosts actually did.

## The invitation
Headline: "People in Falls Church are meeting their neighbors. Want in?" Promise: "Microgrants from $50 to $150 to help Falls Church neighbors host gatherings August–October." It is for residents of the greater Falls Church area. At the time of fetching, "The application period is now closed" and submitted applications were still under review; the live call-to-action is a "Keep me posted" email signup for a future round.

## The grant
Range **$50–$150** per gathering, for events in an August to October window. What is asked of hosts afterward is not stated on the fetched page.

## What the landing page does well
- The headline uses social proof and FOMO in two short sentences: others are already doing this, want in?
- A four-photo carousel (backyard gathering, a painting activity, welcome bags, a community event) shows the range of what counts before any rules appear.
- The page keeps working after close: a status notice plus an email signup, so the closed state still collects the next cohort.
- Grant copy is one sentence with the amounts and the window; nothing else competes with it.
- Visual feel: candid photographs of neighbors, inclusive and informal; colors not stated on the fetched page.
