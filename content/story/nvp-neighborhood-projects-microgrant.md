# Neighborhood Projects Microgrant (Boulder and Denver, CO)

> Neighborhood Village Project offers '$300 for neighbor-led projects in the Boulder & Denver Metro Area': build or plant something together (a food forest, pocket park, emergency cache, bulletin board, pizza oven) and gather around it. Fixed $300, paid within 48 hours of selection.

## The invitation
Headline: "Neighborhood Projects Microgrant". Promise: "$300 for neighbor-led projects in the Boulder & Denver Metro Area". It is for neighbors in that metro area who want to launch a small shared project. At the time of fetching the page read "Application Deadline Has Passed", so no apply button was visible.

## The grant
Fixed **$300**, sent by Venmo, PayPal, or Visa gift card within 48 hours of selection. Deadline was August 25, 2026; projects complete by October 21, 2026; photo and reflection due October 25, 2026. Rules: projects must be "neighbor-led", include a "neighborhood gathering", and spend funds on "supplies and materials".

## What the landing page does well
- Five illustrated project ideas (food forest, pocket park, emergency cache, bulletin board, pizza oven) open the page, so the grant is about making a thing, not just hosting a night.
- International examples of neighbor-led work give small local projects a lineage.
- Paired sections "What You'll Receive" and "What We Ask of You" make the exchange explicit and symmetrical.
- A timeline with every date (deadline, completion, reflection) and a contact for pre-application questions.
- Tone is openly enthusiastic ("stoked", celebratory emoji) without being vague about rules.
- Visual feel: warm, illustrated, neighborhood-scale; colors not stated on the fetched page.
