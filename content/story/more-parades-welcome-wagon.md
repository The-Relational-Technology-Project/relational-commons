# Welcome Wagon (Palouse, WA)

> More Parades, a rural cultural incubator in Palouse, Washington, runs the Welcome Wagon Community Microgrants: 'All the best ideas come from one another :)', small grants for neighbors gathering around special interests and community projects. $250 per the With Neighbors listing, with hands-on practical support alongside the money.

## The invitation
Headline: "Welcome 🌀 Wagon" with the subhead "Microgrant Program" (on the home page, announced as "BIG NEWS!"), and on the program page "Welcome 🌀 Wagon Community Microgrants". Promise: "All the best ideas come from one another :) This program awards small grants to neighbors for gathering around special interests and community projects in Palouse, Washington." The home page button to the program says **Read More**; an apply button and application details were not visible on the fetched pages.

## The grant
The With Neighbors listing gives **$250** and describes a rural program "pairing funding with hands-on practical support"; the More Parades site itself does not state an amount or deadline (not stated on the site). The 2026 grantee pages show funded projects such as Mother Prairie and Coyote puppets for the Palouse Days parade and a Palouse City Drum Corps.

## What the landing page does well
- The program is a set of short pages (Microgrants, 2026 Projects, 2026 People, Credit, Footer) rather than one scroll: the grantees and their projects are the content.
- Each grantee gets a bio in their own voice, so the fund reads like a roster of neighbors, not a pot of money.
- A "Big Thanks" credit page names the funders (With Neighbors, Group Hug, Relational Tech Project, Connective Tissue) and frames the whole thing as "a national neighborhood microgrant blitz".
- The town's own event (Palouse Days parade) anchors every project.
- Visual feel: a Cargo site with a wheat-field wallpaper, a mustard-olive page background (#b5a635), white body copy in Optima and a typewriter display face, emoji icons in the nav. Rural, arty, handmade.
