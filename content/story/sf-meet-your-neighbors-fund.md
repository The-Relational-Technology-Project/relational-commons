# Meet Your Neighbors Fund (San Francisco, CA)

> SF Good Neighbor Week's 'teeny grant to make a big introduction': $100 for individuals to host simple, low-lift gatherings for the folks next door during Good Neighbor Week. Fixed $100, individuals only, backed by Civic Joy Fund and With Neighbors.

## The invitation
Headline: "Meet Your Neighbors Fund". Promise: "A teeny grant to make a big introduction", with the descriptor "A grant to host simple, low-lift gatherings". It is for San Francisco residents applying as individuals (organizations and associations excluded). At the time of fetching: "The application is now closed", so no button text was visible.

## The grant
Fixed **$100**. The application deadline was September 18, 2026; gatherings run September 26 to October 4, 2026 (SF Good Neighbor Week), extended through the end of October. Asked of hosts: introduce neighbors in your building or block with something achievable, "a potluck in your living room, coffee on your driveway". Post-event requirements are not stated on the fetched page.

## What the landing page does well
- "Teeny grant, big introduction" says the amount is small on purpose and makes the smallness part of the charm.
- A problem statement comes first (people don't know the folks next door), then the fund as the answer.
- Examples are deliberately tiny and indoor-friendly for a dense city: living-room potluck, driveway coffee.
- Individuals-only eligibility keeps the money with residents instead of organizations.
- Partner logos (Civic Joy Fund, Good Neighbor Lab, With Neighbors) and the #sfgoodneighborweek hashtag place the fund inside a city-wide week.
- Visual feel: clean, modern, warm neighborhood photography; colors not stated on the fetched page.
