# Hey, Neighbor! Fund (Rome, GA)

> 'When in Rome... get to know your neighbors': a range grant of $50 to $150 for creative, neighborhood-scale gatherings in Rome, Georgia, with a spinning idea wheel on the landing page. The button says 'I'll Host'.

## The invitation
Headline: "WHEN IN ROME... GET TO KNOW YOUR NEIGHBORS". Promise: "Every neighborhood has someone worth meeting. You bring the neighbors; we'll cover $50–$150 of the snacks & supplies." The button says **I'll Host 🙋**. It is for Rome, Georgia residents, with Old East Rome and South Rome named as neighborhoods of focus. The With Neighbors listing credits Bobby Jones as the organizer; the site itself presents as "Hey, Neighbor!".

## The grant
Range **$50–$150** per event, for "snacks & supplies". Applicants propose an event idea and apply through the form. Deadlines and post-event requirements are not stated on the fetched page.

## What the landing page does well
- The headline is a pun on the town's name, which makes the page unmistakably local in five words.
- An interactive spinning wheel generates a gathering idea; the page treats "what would I even do?" as the real barrier and answers it with play.
- Thirty-plus idea cards (5-Minute Talent Show, Front-Yard Movie Night, and more) with a "See all ideas" expander: a "grab bag of ideas", as With Neighbors describes it.
- Button copy is a volunteer's raised hand, emoji included.
- The promise sentence splits labor cleanly: you bring the people, we cover the stuff.
- Visual feel: playful and casual, wheel centered on the page; colors not stated on the fetched page.
