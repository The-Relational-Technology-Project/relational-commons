# Block Party Fund (Los Angeles, CA)

> UTOPIAS, a self-described 'public feelings studio', pays Los Angeles neighbors $150 to throw a block party and 'help map a friendlier LA'. Fixed $150 per host, with a photo and a short story asked for afterward.

## The invitation
The hero is a four-line stacked headline: "WE WANT, TO GIVE YOU, for a party with your, neighbors" (the dollar figure sits between the lines). The one-line promise underneath: "The Utopias Block Party Fund gives $150 to neighbors across Los Angeles to throw a block party. Meet the people on your block, get funded, and help map a friendlier LA." The button says **Apply Today** and repeats down the page. It is for LA residents of any hosting experience; the page names "fast changing LA neighborhoods" as the focus.

## The grant
Fixed **$150** per host. "Applications are open now through Sept 28. Selected hosts throw their parties in October." What is asked back: one photo and "a short story" after the party. A "What counts as a party?" section carries an exclusions list (the specific exclusions were not captured in the fetch).

## What the landing page does well
- Two-beat "Here's how it works" section: *We pay / You party*. The whole mechanic in four words.
- Frames the city-wide effort as a map: every party is a dot on "a friendlier LA", so a host joins something bigger than one block.
- A narrative section titled "Party fever is taking over the city" turns applicants into part of a movement, not a form queue.
- FAQ is headed "Tell me more", keeping the conversational voice even in the fine print.
- Follow-along options (Instagram, newsletter) and a media-outreach section sit below the fold so non-hosts have a next step.
- Visual feel: text-forward and festive (specific colors not stated on the fetched page).
