# Faith Community Care Connector

> Contributed by a community member in Ann Arbor, MI. A prompt for spiritual communities to build a peer-to-peer care app. This tool focuses on mutual aid ('Abundance Exchange', meal trains, ride sharing) and member-led gatherings rather than top-down broadcasts.

Build a mobile-first community web app for [NAME OF FAITH COMMUNITY] in [CITY/LOCATION].

The goal is to create a digital extension of our foyer—a warm, safe space for members to coordinate care, share resources, and organize gatherings without relying on top-down broadcasts.

AESTHETIC & VIBE
- Visuals: Peaceful, grounded, and organic. Use colors from our local landscape.
- Typography: Clean serif headers (welcoming, slightly traditional) paired with highly readable sans-serif body text.
- Interaction: Gentle and human. Using the app should feel like a deep breath, not a dopamine hit.

CORE FEATURES
1. The "Care Board" (Heart of the App): A peer-to-peer board for specific needs (Meal Trains, Rides to service, Help with house projects). Needs are visible to the community, but contact details are private until a helper accepts.
2. The "Abundance Exchange": A marketplace for free sharing (gift economy). Categories for "Have" (extra garden produce, items) and "Seek" (moving boxes, recommendations). 
3. "Service & Stewardship": Low-pressure volunteering. Instead of a roster, show "Open Opportunities" for the upcoming service with one-tap sign-up.
4. "Gatherings": A user-generated feed for members to post pop-up social events (hikes, potlucks) separate from the official calendar.

TECHNICAL & DATA
- Authentication: Simple passwordless login (email magic link) for members only.
- Privacy: No tracking, no ads.
- Mobile First: Must work perfectly on phone browsers.
