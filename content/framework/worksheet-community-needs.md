# What Does Your Community Need?

> Identify which civic need (agency, belonging, or well-being) is most urgent in your community, and what assets already exist to build on.

*Identify which civic need (agency, belonging, or well-being) is most urgent in your community, and what assets already exist to build on.*

*When to use:* The front door. Run this before choosing a recipe.

1. **Listen to Your Community** — Go listen: take a walk, visit a community space, scroll local groups, sit in a coffee shop and eavesdrop. Note which signals you hear.
2. **Identify Patterns** — Which category had the most checks? Which statements resonated most? Which observations felt most acute? What surprised you?
3. **Asset Mapping** — What does your community already have? (You have more than you think.)
4. **Notice What's Already Happening** — Which of the eight patterns do you already see in your community? What's working about those efforts, and what's missing?
5. **Match Needs to Recipes** — Based on your assessment, which recipes might serve your community?

**Closing prompts**
- Your Starting Point: Which recipe calls to you? What pattern could you build on? What's the smallest test you could run in a week?
- Reality Check: Who else needs to be in this conversation? Who might resist, and why? Who's already doing something similar? What could you try tomorrow with $0 and three people?
