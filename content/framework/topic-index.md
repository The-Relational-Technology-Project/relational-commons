# Topic Index

A practical mapping from common questions to specific recipes in this book. Use this when you have a clear situation but aren't sure which recipe fits.

## "I want to get to know my neighbors"

The most common and most important question.

- [Block Party](../reference/block-party.md) — Close your street, bring food, meet people
- [Story-Sharing Potluck](../recipe/story-sharing-potluck-supper.md) — Structured meal with meaningful prompts
- [Porch Conversations](../recipe/porch-conversations.md) — Low-energy practice you can keep up
- [Welcome Wagon](../recipe/welcome-wagon.md) — Greet new neighbors meaningfully
- [Block Stewards](../recipe/block-stewards.md) — Be the connector for your block
