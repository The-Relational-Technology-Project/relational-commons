## What It Is

Ray Oldenburg's foundational concept defining 'third places'—informal public gathering spaces beyond home and work where people congregate, build relationships, and sustain civic culture. Third places include cafes, parks, barbershops, libraries, and community centers that host regular, voluntary, unplanned social interaction.

## Connections

Oldenburg's work complements contemporary placemaking methodology, Eric Klinenberg's social infrastructure research, and approaches to building civic health. It provides conceptual foundation for understanding why places like neighborhood cafes, parks, and community centers matter for community wellbeing.
