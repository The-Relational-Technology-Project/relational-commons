## What It Is

Richard Bartlett's framework explaining how small groups (crews) scale into larger structures (congregations and networks) while maintaining relational depth and decision-making power. This essay articulates the scaling theory behind microsolidarity as an alternative to hierarchical and horizontal-without-structure models.

## Connections

Connects to political theory about federalism and network structures; informs how Big Door Brigade projects coordinate; complements Peter Block's work on community-wide conversations.
