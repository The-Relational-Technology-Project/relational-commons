## What It Is

Founded in 1973 by veterans of civil rights and anti-war movements, the Midwest Academy has spent 50 years training organizers in concrete organizing skills, movement methodology, and democratic leadership. Has trained 50,000+ activists in base-building, campaign waging, and power-building.

## Connections

Shares Training for Change's cross-movement training approach. Publishes "Organizing for Social Change" manual widely used alongside Community Tool Box resources.
