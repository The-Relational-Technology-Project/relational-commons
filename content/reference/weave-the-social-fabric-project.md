## What It Is

The Aspen Institute's initiative to strengthen social connection and address social fragmentation at scale. Weave identifies and amplifies local 'weavers'—people who intentionally build connections across differences—and develops frameworks for community-based social cohesion work.

## Connections

Shares DNA with microsolidarity and relational organizing approaches; provides institutional infrastructure for scalable community connection work.
