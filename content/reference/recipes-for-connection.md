# Recipes for Connection

## What It Is

Surgeon General Vivek Murthy's initiative providing creative ideas for gathering over food as a response to America's epidemic of loneliness and isolation. The guide offers practical suggestions for hosts and community organizers to strengthen relationships and reduce social isolation through food-centered gatherings.

## Connections

Recipes for Connection aligns with Community Plate's story sharing suppers, potluck organizing, and broader food-based community building. It represents federal-level support for grassroots community connection practices and complements local organizing efforts.
