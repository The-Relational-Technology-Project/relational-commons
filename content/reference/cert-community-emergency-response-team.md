## What It Is

A FEMA-sponsored program (conceived 1985 by LA Fire Dept, national since 1993) training volunteers in disaster preparedness and response. Operates in all 50 states plus tribal nations and territories, using consistent training to prepare neighbors to help themselves, families, and communities during disasters.

## Connections

Foundational to Building Resilient Neighbourhoods and Neighbor-to-Neighbor disaster preparedness work. Complements mutual aid approaches.
