# Welcome Groups Training Manual

> A comprehensive volunteer training resource from Together Project for refugee integration. The manual equips volunteer groups with knowledge and skills to support newly arrived refugee families in Canadian communities.

## What It Is

A comprehensive volunteer training resource from Together Project for refugee integration. The manual equips volunteer groups with knowledge and skills to support newly arrived refugee families in Canadian communities.

## How It Works

The training manual guides volunteer groups of 3-5 people through a structured process of matching with refugee families over 3-6 months. Volunteers learn to assess family needs, provide conversational English practice, help navigate healthcare and employment services, support youth school transitions, and facilitate community orientation and social activities. The program recommends approximately 1 hour of weekly interaction (online or in-person) shared among group members, totaling 3-4 hours monthly per volunteer. Together Project provides ongoing support and resources, and can connect volunteers who speak Arabic, Farsi, French, Dari, Kurdish, Pashto, Somali, Spanish, Swahili, or Tigrinya as cultural ambassadors to bridge language and cultural gaps. Matching considers location, family size, shared interests, volunteer skills, family composition, and volunteer availability.

## Who 'S Done It

Together Project has operated since 2016, initially responding to the arrival of Syrian refugee families in Toronto. The program now operates in Toronto, Mississauga, Vancouver, Winnipeg, and Moncton, and has recently expanded across Canada to Atlantic Canada, the Prairies, and the West Coast. Volunteer groups have successfully supported refugee families from over 25 different countries, with documented positive outcomes in terms of social integration, employment pathways, and community belonging. The program demonstrates that structured volunteer-community partnerships create meaningful, long-term support networks for newcomers.

## Connections

Together Project's model builds on social integration theory and volunteer-based community support models. It connects to broader refugee welcome movements across North America while adapting to the Canadian context. The approach complements formal settlement services by providing informal social capital and community belonging—elements research shows are crucial for successful long-term integration.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 3-6 months per match; 1 hour weekly per volunteer (3-4 hours monthly)
- *People*: Groups of 3-5 volunteers; refugee families; cultural ambassadors for language support
- *Space*: Flexible (in-person or online interactions based on preference)
- *Materials*: Training manual; resources on settlement topics; communication tools
- *Skills*: Patience, cultural humility, active listening, basic problem-solving; no prior experience required
- *Energy Level*: Moderate; sustained, consistent engagement over months rather than intensive
