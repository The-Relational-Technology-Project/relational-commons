## What It Is

A training and capacity-building organization (founded 1992) that develops organizing skills across movements and issues. The flagship Organizing Skills Institute (OSI) brings together community, labor, and electoral organizers for cross-movement learning in base-building, campaign waging, and contesting for power.

## Connections

Works alongside Midwest Academy and IAF in providing sustained organizing training. Shares pedagogy with Community Tool Box and Beautiful Trouble's participatory learning models.
