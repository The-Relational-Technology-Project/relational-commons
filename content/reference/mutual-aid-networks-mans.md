## What It Is

A networked cooperative model that combines timebanking, cooperative lending, and community currencies to create location-specific solidarity economies. Originally developed in Madison, Wisconsin, MANs pool community capacity and reward socially and environmentally useful work through democratic participation and exchange systems.

## How It Works

Members earn and spend 'MAN hours' by helping one another—every hour of help provides one hour of credit usable throughout the network. The system integrates timebanking as the primary exchange mechanism while also facilitating cooperative lending, community savings pools, and shared manufacturing or community spaces. Participants access leadership training and participate in democratic decision-making about resource allocation and community priorities. The Madison MAN Cooperative serves as the home base and founding member of HUMANs (Humans United in Mutual Aid Networks), a global cooperative network designed for mutual learning and support.

## Who 'S Done It

Madison Mutual Aid Network in Madison, Wisconsin (founded by Stephanie Rearick); Dane County Time Bank (2,800 members); HUMANs global network; communities adopting the model across North America and internationally.

## Connections

Evolved from 20 years of local economic development work in Wisconsin, building on Madison HOURS currency (1990s) and expanding from the Dane County Time Bank model. Connected to broader timebanking movement, cooperative economics, and community currency initiatives. Part of emerging networked cooperativism and solidarity economy frameworks.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: Ongoing weekly/monthly participation; initial training 2-4 hours
- *People*: 15-50+ active participants per fledgling network; diverse skill sets and backgrounds
- *Space*: Community hub for coordination, meetings, and resource sharing (cooperative workspace, community center, or rotating locations)
- *Materials*: Digital or paper-based time ledger system; currency or credit tracking system; communication platform (email, app, or forum)
- *Skills*: Facilitation, cooperative governance, conflict resolution, basic accounting, communication
- *Energy Level*: Medium to high—requires consistent participation and democratic engagement
