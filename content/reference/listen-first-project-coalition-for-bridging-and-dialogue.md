## What It Is

A coalition movement bringing together practitioners, organizations, and researchers working on dialogue, bridging, and social connection. The Listen First Project serves as a hub for the bridging movement, sharing resources, amplifying impact, and coordinating across diverse approaches.

## Connections

Hub organization connecting all bridging approaches; informs how dialogue and mutual aid work can reinforce each other.
