# Building Relational, Place-Based Technology

> An approach to technology development centered on building with communities rather than for them, emphasizing local scale, relational metrics over growth metrics, and intentional friction that generates creative energy and meaning.

## What It Is

An approach to technology development centered on building with communities rather than for them, emphasizing local scale, relational metrics over growth metrics, and intentional friction that generates creative energy and meaning.

## Connections

Offers technology alternative to connection grift; informs decisions about digital tools for community organizing.
