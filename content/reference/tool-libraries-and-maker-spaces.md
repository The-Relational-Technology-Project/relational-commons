# Tool Libraries and Maker Spaces

## What It Is

Community-based libraries and spaces where neighbors share tools, equipment, and knowledge. Rather than individuals buying expensive tools they use occasionally, communities collectively own tools, machines, and maker equipment that members borrow, with library model membership enabling both access and community connection.

## How It Works

Membership-based model ($20-100/year) provides borrowing access to hand tools, power tools, specialized equipment, and workspace. Tool libraries track inventory through library systems. Maker spaces extend this to include computers, 3D printers, welding equipment, woodworking tools, craft supplies. Many offer classes and workshops teaching tool safety and specific skills. Community forms around shared making and learning. Spaces often feature community workshops where members help each other troubleshoot projects.

## Who 'S Done It

Tool lending libraries grew from 40 in 2013 to 60+ by 2015 with continued growth. Shareable and the Tool Library Network support the movement. Maker spaces proliferated as libraries and community organizations recognized maker culture's appeal. Examples exist in most major cities and many smaller communities.

## Connections

Emerged from DIY culture, sustainability concerns about consumption, and library concepts adapted to material goods. Connected to little free libraries and pantries, buy nothing groups, and community wealth models. Part of maker culture and participatory learning movements.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: Borrow periods typically 1-2 weeks; class participation variable
- *People*: Tool librarians/staff, volunteer board, community members borrowing and sharing knowledge
- *Space*: Dedicated library/studio space, shelving/storage, work tables, class space
- *Materials*: Tools, equipment, tracking/management software, safety equipment, teaching materials
- *Skills*: Tool knowledge, library management, teaching, safety training, community facilitation
- *Energy Level*: Moderate—hands-on making, learning, community engagement
