## What It Is

An Indigenous-led organization headquartered in Rapid City, South Dakota, working toward Indigenous sovereignty and self-determination. NDN Collective combines grassroots organizing, philanthropy, lending, narrative change, community programs, and policy advocacy to advance Indigenous-centered justice.

## Connections

Part of broader indigenous sovereignty, environmental justice, and decolonization movements. Relates to indigenous mutual aid networks, reparations movements, and indigenous-centered philanthropy.
