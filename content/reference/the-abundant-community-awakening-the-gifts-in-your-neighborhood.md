## What It Is

A book by John McKnight and Peter Block presenting Asset-Based Community Development (ABCD)—a paradigm shift from viewing communities through deficit (what's broken, what's needed) to asset (what gifts exist, what's possible). ABCD emphasizes that communities are built from the assets and gifts of their citizens, not from outside expertise or resource programs.

## Connections

Foundational to Peter Block's broader community building work; informs microsolidarity emphasis on gifts and reciprocity; complements mutual aid's focus on community care capacity.
