# Relational Design

> Some of the work of Victor Udoewa, who has written papers on relational design.  The first two links are a video and paper about how futures have been practiced by communities, especially by oppressed people and decolonial movements, and how relational design inverts the common Eurocentric design practices focused on outcomes and motivated by optimism and hope with ones that center relationships and are born of hopelessness and impossibility.  The last link explores how the design process might change when you invite the people for whom you are designing to be active participants in the design process itself through sustained dialogue.
