## What It Is

Indigenous approaches to community care centered on interconnection, mutual support, and reciprocity rather than accumulation. These traditions recognize all community members as valued throughout their life stages, emphasize giving away wealth to strengthen community, and weave care into ceremonial, artistic, and daily practices.

## Connections

Part of broader indigenous sovereignty and decolonization movements. Relates to gift economies, mutual aid, food justice, and environmental stewardship. Connects to seven generations principle and indigenous futures frameworks.
