## What It Is

A digital archive and celebration of Bernard DeKoven's legacy, providing access to his extensive writings, games, videos, and insights on playfulness as a transformative force. Functions as both a resource library for communities seeking to embrace play and an invitation to explore one's own capacity for creative joy.

## Connections

Directly extends Bernard DeKoven's influence on contemporary play movements. Relates to initiatives like Come Out and Play Festival, New Games Movement legacy work, and community-based play facilitation. Connects academic play theory with accessible community practice.
