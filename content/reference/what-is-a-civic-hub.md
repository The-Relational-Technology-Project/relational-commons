## What It Is

A guide from Trust for Civic Life explaining civic hubs as embedded community groups that act as bridge builders and connectors. Hubs coordinate resources and empower other groups to solve shared problems while operating as trusted, behind-the-scenes connectors in their communities.

## Connections

Civic hubs align with the 'Action Over Dialogue' research showing that community coordination and shared work builds trust. The model connects to community organizing, network weaving, and infrastructure development for civic participation.
