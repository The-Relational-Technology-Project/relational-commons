# WHO Age-Friendly Cities Framework

> A global framework developed by the World Health Organization defining eight interconnected domains for making cities inclusive and health-promoting for people of all ages. Built on UN Principles of Independence, Participation, Care, Self-fulfillment, and Dignity.

## What It Is

A global framework developed by the World Health Organization defining eight interconnected domains for making cities inclusive and health-promoting for people of all ages. Built on UN Principles of Independence, Participation, Care, Self-fulfillment, and Dignity.

## Connections

Informs Building Resilient Neighbourhoods and Neighbor-to-Neighbor programs. Complements Encore.org and Generations United's intergenerational vision.
