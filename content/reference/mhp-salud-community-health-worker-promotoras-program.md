# MHP Salud: Community Health Worker & Promotoras Program

## What It Is

An organization (founded 1983) training and supporting Community Health Workers (Promotores/Promotoras de Salud) who are trusted community members providing health education, resources connection, and trusted care in underserved communities. Focus on Latino/Hispanic populations across four states with nationwide technical assistance.

## Connections

Complements Community Tool Box and RHIhub approaches. Often integrated with Faith in Action and parish-based programs.
