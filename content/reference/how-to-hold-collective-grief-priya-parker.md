## What It Is

A framework by gatherings expert Priya Parker for creating intentional spaces to process collective loss. Parker argues that Western culture lacks language and rituals for shared grief, and that by purposefully holding collective grief in community, we transform individual pain into collective resilience.

## Connections

Part of broader grief justice and community care movements. Relates to gathering practices, collective healing work, and movements around death positivity. Connects to restorative justice, compassionate communities, and transformative organizing.
