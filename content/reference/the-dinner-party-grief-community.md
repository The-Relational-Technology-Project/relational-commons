# The Dinner Party (Grief Community)

> A peer-led grief support community for people ages 18-45 who've experienced significant loss. The Dinner Party brings young grievers together around shared meals in intimate settings to normalize grief conversation and build lasting support relationships. Now in 150+ cities.

## What It Is

A peer-led grief support community for people ages 18-45 who've experienced significant loss. The Dinner Party brings young grievers together around shared meals in intimate settings to normalize grief conversation and build lasting support relationships. Now in 150+ cities.

## How It Works

Visit thedinnerparty.org and express interest in a Table (their term for a grief support group). You'll be matched with 6-8 peers navigating grief. Tables meet every 2-6 weeks, in-person (shared meal at someone's home) or virtually. No formal agenda: peers share food and talk about how loss impacts their lives, relationships, work. They also offer a Buddy System with hand-matched one-to-one connections. The atmosphere is intentionally casual and non-clinical — good food, real conversation — while holding space for grief's complexity. No one is expected to 'move on' or follow a timeline.

## Who 'S Done It

Rey Sosa lost his mother to COVID-19 at age 26. He joined an in-person grief group but was the only person under 50, the only man, surrounded by people grieving jobs, not parents. When he found The Dinner Party, he found peers at the same life stage. Elena Lopez Del Carril was matched with someone whose grief story mirrored hers — both lost fathers weeks apart, both the youngest in their families. That buddy connection, forged across continents (US and UK), became a lifeline. Founded by Carla Fernandez in 2010 with a paella dinner in LA.

## Connections

Draws from peer support traditions and the understanding that informal community care often heals better than clinical settings. Connects to movements for mental health democratization, destigmatization of grief, and feminist care practices. Builds on dinner party culture and the power of shared meals to create vulnerability. Pairs with Compassionate Communities movement and practices like talking circles.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 2-3 hours per gathering every 2-6 weeks, ongoing indefinitely
- *People*: 6-8 peer participants, trained facilitator/peer leader
- *Space*: Home, community kitchen, restaurant private room, or virtual
- *Materials*: Food/drinks for shared meal
- *Skills*: Peer support, active listening, comfort with grief conversation
- *Energy Level*: Medium — emotional engagement, sitting, conversation
