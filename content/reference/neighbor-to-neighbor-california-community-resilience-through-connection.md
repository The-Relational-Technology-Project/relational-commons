## What It Is

A $10 million California Volunteers Network (funded FY 2022-23) strengthening communities through social connection. Brings neighbors together for community engagement on climate action, disaster preparedness, and local needs, with campaigns like "Meet Six Neighbors" based on research connecting neighbor relationships to health and resilience.

## Connections

Implements WHO Age-Friendly and Building Resilient Neighbourhoods models in California context. Operationalizes CERT and disaster preparedness through relationship-building.
