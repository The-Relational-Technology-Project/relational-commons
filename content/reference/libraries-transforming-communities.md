## What It Is

A flagship initiative from the American Library Association designed to position public libraries as core community leaders and change agents. The program strengthens librarians' capacity to engage with their communities, address civic challenges, and serve as facilitators of inclusive public dialogue.

## Connections

Relates to broader library innovation movements emphasizing civic purpose and community centeredness. Connects with initiatives like PLA's Civic & Community Engagement toolkit and models of libraries as 'trusted community anchors.' Also aligns with broader democratic renewal and community resilience frameworks.
