# The Connection Opportunity: How Social Connection Reduces Polarization

> More in Common research examining the empirical link between social connection and reduced polarization. The report demonstrates that people with diverse social networks and strong community bonds are significantly less likely to adopt extreme positions or adopt tribal worldviews

## What It Is

More in Common research examining the empirical link between social connection and reduced polarization. The report demonstrates that people with diverse social networks and strong community bonds are significantly less likely to adopt extreme positions or adopt tribal worldviews.

## Connections

Provides empirical grounding for Living Room Conversations, microsolidarity, and mutual aid approaches; supports rationale for community-building work.
