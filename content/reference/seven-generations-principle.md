## What It Is

An indigenous decision-making framework originating from the Haudenosaunee Confederacy's Great Law of Peace (estimated 1142-1500 AD). The principle teaches that decisions made today should ensure a sustainable world for the next seven generations—roughly spanning from great-grandparents through great-grandchildren.

## Connections

Part of broader indigenous governance frameworks and environmental justice movements. Relates to long-term sustainability thinking, intergenerational justice, and decolonial practices. Connects to movements addressing climate change, land restoration, and indigenous futurism.
