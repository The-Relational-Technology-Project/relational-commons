# Encore.org: Purposeful Aging and Encore Careers

> An organization (founded 1997 as Civic Ventures) that harnesses talents and experience of people 50+ to tackle society's urgent challenges. Pioneered the concept of "encore careers"—distinctive career paths in second half of life combining money, meaning, and social impact.

## What It Is

An organization (founded 1997 as Civic Ventures) that harnesses talents and experience of people 50+ to tackle society's urgent challenges. Pioneered the concept of "encore careers"—distinctive career paths in second half of life combining money, meaning, and social impact.

## Connections

Complements AARP Experience Corps and WHO Age-Friendly approach. Partners with Generations United on intergenerational programming.
