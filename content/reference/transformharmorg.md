# TransformHarm.org

> A resource hub for ending violence and promoting restorative justice, created by Mariame Kaba. TransformHarm.org curates and organizes materials—articles, media, curricula, and resources—on alternative approaches to justice, harm prevention, and community accountability.

## What It Is

A resource hub for ending violence and promoting restorative justice, created by Mariame Kaba. TransformHarm.org curates and organizes materials—articles, media, curricula, and resources—on alternative approaches to justice, harm prevention, and community accountability.

## Connections

Part of broader restorative justice, transformative justice, and abolition movements. Relates to community accountability frameworks, conflict resolution practices, and alternatives to incarceration. Connects to disability justice, harm reduction, and community care movements.
