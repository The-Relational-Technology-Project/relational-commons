# An Invitation to a New Civic Future

> A vision for civic renewal centered on community agency, trust-based relationships, and local stewardship, emphasizing that renewal must be rooted in people who live in places and accountable to their neighbors rather than operating from afar.

## What It Is

A vision for civic renewal centered on community agency, trust-based relationships, and local stewardship, emphasizing that renewal must be rooted in people who live in places and accountable to their neighbors rather than operating from afar.

## Connections

Articulates the positive vision underlying critiques of professionalization and grift; informs values and principles for all community-building work.
