# Foundation for Intentional Community (FIC)

> A registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with 35+ years of service supporting the intentional community movement. FIC champions social, ecological, and economic justice through supporting and growing cooperative culture and intentional communities of all types.

## What It Is

A registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with 35+ years of service supporting the intentional community movement. FIC champions social, ecological, and economic justice through supporting and growing cooperative culture and intentional communities of all types.

## Connections

Part of broader cooperative, housing justice, and alternative living movements. Relates to ecovillage networks, land trust movements, and social change organizing. Connects to practices of shared resources, participatory governance, and community self-determination.
