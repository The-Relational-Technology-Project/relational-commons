A comprehensive guide to participation tools and methods for involving communities in planning for health-promoting land use and transportation, with specific focus on lower-income and underserved communities. Includes design charrettes, community image surveys, and virtual engagement tools.

Operationalizes Maine Visioning Handbook methods. Pairs with IAF and Faith in Action relationship-building approaches for genuine community input.
