## What It Is

A mutual aid health practice where acupuncture treatment is provided in shared community rooms on a sliding scale, eliminating financial barriers to healthcare. Patients sit together in a large, well-ventilated healing space, participate in collective healing, and pay what they can afford—those paying more support those paying less in a trust-based model of shared responsibility.

## How It Works

Patients receive acupuncture in a community treatment room alongside 8-12 other patients rather than in private rooms. Sliding scale pricing typically ranges from $10-70 depending on income and ability to pay, with many clinics operating on trust without income verification. Patients are encouraged to pay generously if they can to support the model. Some programs include 'pay-it-forward' treatments donated by supporters for people unable to afford even the minimum cost. Treatment protocols are standardized for community settings.

## Who 'S Done It

Community acupuncture clinics operate nationwide: Albany Community Acupuncture (NY), Little Pine (Portland), Boston Acupuncture Project, Brooklyn Acupuncture Project, Acupuncture for the People, Open Care Community Acupuncture (NYC). The practice is expanding in response to healthcare access gaps and aligns with harm reduction and community health movements.

## Connections

Emerged from the acupuncture community as a response to health equity concerns and barriers to traditional medicine access. Connects to mutual aid health practices, sliding scale economics, community healthcare, trust-based models, and the broader mutual aid movement. Related to MHP Salud, Compassionate Communities, and disability justice frameworks.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 1 hour treatment sessions; ongoing weekly or bi-weekly scheduling
- *People*: Licensed acupuncturist(s), admin staff, patient community, donors for pay-it-forward
- *Space*: Large room with recliners/chairs, quiet, comfortable temperature, accessibility
- *Materials*: Acupuncture needles, sterilization equipment, intake forms, waiver documents
- *Skills*: Licensed acupuncture practice, community facilitation, trust-based economics management
- *Energy Level*: Low to moderate—sitting/lying still, meditative healing space
