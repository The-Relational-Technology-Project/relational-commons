## What It Is

A network of locally-rooted organizing chapters that build connection and reduce polarization through shared service work. Rather than structured dialogue, Brickyard brings people together around concrete projects (food distribution, mutual aid, community care) that reveal common humanity and values.

## Connections

Complements dialogue-based approaches like Living Room Conversations; aligned with mutual aid and service-based organizing models.
