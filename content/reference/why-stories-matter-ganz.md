# Why Stories Matter (Marshall Ganz)

> Ganz's short, canonical essay on the craft of public narrative — why social change runs on stories of self, us, and now, written for Sojourners in 2009.

The most-shared single piece of writing on public narrative: Marshall Ganz's 2009 Sojourners essay 'Why Stories Matter: The Art and Craft of Social Change.' In a few pages it lays out the case that social movements move on narrative — that stories communicate values through emotion where facts cannot, that leaders have a responsibility to author a public account of themselves ('if we don't author our own story, others will'), and that the story of self, the story of us, and the story of now together turn values into agency.

A good first send to a neighbor who asks what this storytelling business has to do with getting the crosswalk fixed. Pairs with the Public Narrative recipe on this shelf and the worksheets in the Organizer's Handbook.
