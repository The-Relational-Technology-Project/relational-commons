## What It Is

Eric Klinenberg's framework emphasizing that democratic societies thrive through investment in 'social infrastructure'—public institutions, shared spaces, and community gathering places where crucial connections form and civic life flourishes. Social infrastructure includes libraries, parks, schools, community centers, and the public spaces (sidewalks, courtyards) that connect them.

## Connections

Klinenberg's work extends Oldenburg's third place concept, emphasizing public institutions alongside informal gathering spaces. It provides research-backed framework for advocating investment in placemaking, community centers, libraries, and public parks. It connects to civic health measurement and community resilience frameworks.
