## What It Is

An economic development model that transforms local economies through democratic ownership and control of assets. Rather than wealth flowing out of communities to distant shareholders, community wealth building systematically connects and scales institution-building practices (cooperatives, land trusts, public banking, ethical procurement) to create broadly-shared prosperity, racial equity, and ecological sustainability.

## Who 'S Done It

Scotland has adopted a national community wealth building approach with all 32 local governments developing CWB action plans and a government Minister for Community Wealth. In the US, cities and regions are adopting CWB frameworks. The model is being adapted internationally and has influenced economic policy discussions globally.

## Connections

Articulated by The Democracy Collaborative in 2005, drawing on cooperative economics, community land trust movements, public banking theory, and critiques of extractive capitalism. Connected to mutual aid economics, cooperatives, community ownership models, and Indigenous economic practices. Part of broader movement toward economic democracy.
