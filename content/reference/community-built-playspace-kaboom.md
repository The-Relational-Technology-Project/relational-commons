# Community-Built Playspace (KABOOM!)

> A community design and build process where neighbors — especially kids — collaborate with expert facilitators to design, plan, and construct a new playground together over a concentrated building period. 17,000+ playspaces built engaging 1.5 million community members.

## What It Is

A community design and build process where neighbors — especially kids — collaborate with expert facilitators to design, plan, and construct a new playground together over a concentrated building period. 17,000+ playspaces built engaging 1.5 million community members.

## How It Works

A community partner applies to KABOOM!. If selected, a Community Mobilization Manager leads a 5-12 week planning phase centering community input, especially children. Kids and adults meet in workshops to dream about their playspace. Community input is collected through surveys, meetings, and design sessions. KABOOM! coordinates materials. On build days (typically four concentrated days), volunteers — neighbors, businesses, families — construct the playground together. Expert Build Managers ensure safety. The building is celebratory with work stations for different skill levels. Once complete, the community takes ownership.

## Who 'S Done It

At Detroit's St. Jude Family Childcare Center, a KABOOM! build transformed a vacant lot into a beloved playground. A local resident began opening and closing it on weekends so neighbors could use it. The Discover partnership exemplifies sustained commitment: over 30 years, 13,000 volunteers have donated 75,000+ hours to 50+ builds. Communities consistently report that the build itself — not just the playground — catalyzes lasting civic engagement.

## Connections

Emerges from participatory design, co-design methodology, and the recognition that spaces built by communities are maintained with greater pride. Connects to asset-based community development, child-centered planning, and tactical urbanism. Reflects broader movements for equitable public space access and the understanding that play is essential to community wellbeing.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 5-12 weeks planning, 4 concentrated build days
- *People*: 50-200+ volunteers, children and adults from community, KABOOM! facilitators
- *Space*: Vacant lot, park area (2,000-5,000 sq ft)
- *Materials*: Playground equipment, lumber, hardware, safety surfacing, tools, paint
- *Skills*: Facilitation, project management, construction (various levels welcomed)
- *Energy Level*: High — physical construction, collaborative energy
