# Men's Council Program (Journeymen)

> A structured deep listening circle program where men gather regularly to speak and listen truthfully with vulnerability and integrity. Guided by trained facilitators, these councils create safe space for men to share challenges, build mutual understanding, and support one another

## What It Is

A structured deep listening circle program where men gather regularly to speak and listen truthfully with vulnerability and integrity. Guided by trained facilitators, these councils create safe space for men to share challenges, build mutual understanding, and support one another without judgment or attempts to fix.

## How It Works

Men's Council typically meets monthly or bi-weekly in groups of 8-12. Facilitators open with grounding practices—often breathing exercises or centering moments. The council uses council protocols: participants sit in circle; a talking piece or clear turn-taking ensures everyone speaks without interruption; listeners practice deep listening without planning responses, fixing problems, or offering unsolicited advice. Topics emerge naturally from participants' lives—relationships, fatherhood, grief, purpose, masculinity, challenges—rather than following agendas. Facilitators establish ground rules emphasizing confidentiality, respect, and non-judgment. Sessions typically last 90 minutes to 2 hours. The consistency of regular meeting creates trust that deepens over months. Journeymen provides training for facilitators in the council process, shadow practices for new leaders, and support for establishing groups.

## Who 'S Done It

Journeymen is a 501(c)(3) based on Vashon Island, Washington, founded on principles of mentorship and social-emotional learning. Their Men's Council program has facilitated groups across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Participants report experiencing safer emotional expression, deeper connection with themselves and others, and healthier ways of expressing emotions. The organization has adapted council work for boys (Boys Council) and school settings (CIRCLES program).

## Connections

Men's Council draws from Indigenous circle traditions, contemporary peacebuilding practices, and men's movement work. It connects to Circle practices used in Art of Hosting and restorative justice circles. The deep listening protocol reflects dialogue traditions and therapeutic group work. It represents a application of ancient council practices to contemporary men's social-emotional development.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 90 minutes to 2 hours per session; monthly or bi-weekly recurring
- *People*: 8-12 men per circle; trained facilitator essential
- *Space*: Comfortable room where people can sit in circle; adequate privacy and minimal distractions
- *Materials*: Talking piece (optional), ground rules poster, refreshments, comfortable seating
- *Skills*: Deep listening, non-judgment, emotional openness, basic group facilitation, comfort with silence
- *Energy Level*: Moderate; emotionally engaged but not physically demanding
