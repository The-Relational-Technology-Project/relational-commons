## What It Is

Community gardens are shared plots of land where neighbors collectively grow food and flowers while building connection across demographic differences. Research demonstrates gardens function as essential social infrastructure, cultivating three forms of social capital (bonding, bridging, linking) that strengthen community resilience and well-being.

## Connections

Relates to food justice movements, urban agriculture, place-based community organizing, and environmental justice. Part of broader infrastructure movements reconceptualizing public space as sites for community building. Connects to practices emphasizing mutual aid and resource sharing.
