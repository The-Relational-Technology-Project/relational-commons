# Generations United: Intergenerational Shared Sites

> A framework and toolkit from Generations United for creating settings where children, youth, and older adults participate in services and programs together. Reduces isolation, creates mutual benefit, and improves cost-efficiency through shared space and resources.

## What It Is

A framework and toolkit from Generations United for creating settings where children, youth, and older adults participate in services and programs together. Reduces isolation, creates mutual benefit, and improves cost-efficiency through shared space and resources.

## Connections

Foundational framework for AARP Experience Corps and Encore.org initiatives. Informs WHO Age-Friendly Cities work.
