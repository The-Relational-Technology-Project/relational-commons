## What It Is

A leadership framework that teaches people to craft three interconnected stories — Story of Self, Story of Us, Story of Now — to motivate others to join in action for change. It recognizes that humans are moved by stories that touch emotion, create empathy, and reveal shared values.

## How It Works

Three components that build on each other. Story of Self: tell a specific challenge you faced, the choice you made, and the outcome. This reveals the values that called you to leadership. Story of Us: tell a specific story about specific people at a specific time that reminds everyone of shared values. Story of Now: communicate an urgent challenge to those shared values that demands action. The power emerges when you weave all three in sequence, moving listeners from personal connection to shared identity to collective responsibility. Practice in pairs, then small groups, then larger audiences. Each story should take 2-3 minutes.

## Who 'S Done It

Marshall Ganz learned this through 16 years with the United Farm Workers under Cesar Chavez, including the historic 300-mile march from Delano to Sacramento in 1966. As a Harvard Kennedy School lecturer, he trained Obama 2008 campaign organizers who used the framework to mobilize unprecedented grassroots participation. Today, organizers, nonprofit leaders, and movement builders worldwide use Public Narrative. The framework works because people don't join movements through argument — they join because they're moved by story and feel part of something larger.

## Connections

Draws on centuries of oral tradition, religious teaching, and grassroots organizing. Ganz synthesized it from farm workers and civil rights movements. Part of his broader 'strategic capacity' framework. Often taught alongside one-on-one conversations, house meetings, and action-based learning. Complements Skillshares and community meetings by giving people words for why they're gathering.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 2-3 hours for initial training, ongoing practice to refine
- *People*: Facilitator trained in Public Narrative, group of 5-50
- *Space*: Quiet, safe space for listening and practice
- *Materials*: Writing materials, sometimes recorded examples
- *Skills*: Storytelling, vulnerability, listening, facilitation
- *Energy Level*: Medium — emotional but energizing
