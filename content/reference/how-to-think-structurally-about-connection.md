# How to Think Structurally About Connection

> A framework for diagnosing why communities lack connection by analyzing three interdependent dimensions: civic infrastructure (physical third places), participation mechanisms (barriers and pathways), and relationships (bonding and bridging social capital).

## What It Is

A framework for diagnosing why communities lack connection by analyzing three interdependent dimensions: civic infrastructure (physical third places), participation mechanisms (barriers and pathways), and relationships (bonding and bridging social capital).

## Connections

Provides diagnostic framework for city membership work and informs strategies for cultivating civic infrastructure investments.
