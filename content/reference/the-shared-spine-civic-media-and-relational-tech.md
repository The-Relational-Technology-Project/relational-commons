# The Shared Spine: Civic Media and Relational Tech

> Three interlocking frameworks: the Hierarchy of Information Needs tells you what to prioritize; the Cookbook's three civic needs (Agency, Belonging, Well-Being) tell you what information should do for a person; RT's Agency, Belonging, Trust tells you what to measure as it works.

Three frameworks interlock as the conceptual backbone where civic media meets relational tech:

- The **Hierarchy of Information Needs** (News Futures) tells you *what to prioritize*: survival, then connection, then abstract.
- The Civic Media Cookbook's **three civic needs** (Agency, Belonging, Well-Being) tell you *what the information should do for a person*.
- Relational Tech's **Agency, Belonging, Trust** tells you *what to measure as it works*.

Agency and Belonging appear in two of the three independently — a real convergence, not a coincidence of vocabulary. Both traditions reach for the same metaphor: the Cookbook opens on 'good information requires good soil, good technique, good company'; relational tech talks about cultivating 'relational soil.'
