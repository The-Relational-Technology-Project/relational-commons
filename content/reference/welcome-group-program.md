# Welcome Group Program

> Together Project's volunteer matching initiative that connects groups of local volunteers with refugee newcomer families. The program facilitates social integration and settlement support through sustained community relationships over 3-6 months.

## What It Is

Together Project's volunteer matching initiative that connects groups of local volunteers with refugee newcomer families. The program facilitates social integration and settlement support through sustained community relationships over 3-6 months.

## Who 'S Done It

Together Project has matched hundreds of volunteer groups with refugee families across five Canadian cities since 2016. The initiative gained momentum with Syrian refugee sponsorships in Canada and has since supported newcomers from over 25 countries. Impact studies document that Welcome Group participants report increased social capital, better employment outcomes, and stronger community belonging. Volunteers report personal transformation through the relationships formed.

## Connections

This approach reflects best practices in volunteer-led settlement support and builds on the sponsorship model developed for refugee resettlement in Canada. It aligns with peer support and social capital literature demonstrating that informal community networks significantly enhance integration outcomes.
