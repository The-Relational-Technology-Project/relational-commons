## What It Is

Trust for Civic Life's research report presenting findings from a survey of 584 rural community leaders and residents about what builds trust and belonging. The report challenges assumptions that dialogue alone strengthens social bonds, instead showing that shared action is more effective.

## Connections

This finding supports Trust for Civic Life's Civic Hub model and the organization's support for Civic Experiment grants that prioritize action-oriented initiatives. It relates to broader debates about engagement strategies in polarized communities.
