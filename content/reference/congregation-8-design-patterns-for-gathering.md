# Congregation: 8 Design Patterns for Gathering

> Richard Bartlett's guide to designing large gatherings (congregations of 30-200+ people) that maintain relational depth, shared decision-making, and meaningful participation. This resource provides eight reusable design patterns that have proven effective in diverse contexts.

## What It Is

Richard Bartlett's guide to designing large gatherings (congregations of 30-200+ people) that maintain relational depth, shared decision-making, and meaningful participation. This resource provides eight reusable design patterns that have proven effective in diverse contexts.

## How It Works

The eight patterns address: (1) arrival and welcome; (2) creating psychological safety and establishing group norms; (3) structuring participation so everyone can contribute (not just dominant voices); (4) decision-making processes that are clear and inclusive; (5) managing conflict or dissent within large groups; (6) distributing power and facilitating peer-to-peer connection; (7) maintaining continuity and collective memory; (8) closing and next steps. Each pattern includes multiple design variations, examples from different contexts, and troubleshooting for common challenges. The patterns work together to create conditions where large groups can maintain the relational quality of small groups.

## Who 'S Done It

These patterns have been applied in convergence networks, activist conferences, cooperative business gatherings, and community organizing spaces. One example: a 150-person housing rights gathering in Chicago used the patterns to structure both plenary sessions and breakout work, enabling meaningful contribution from members with varying experience levels while reaching concrete decisions about campaign strategy.

## Connections

Draws from facilitation traditions, participatory democracy practices, and relational organizing; connects to Peter Block's conversations framework and Living Room Conversations structure.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 1-3 days for congregation gathering, 20+ hours facilitation prep
- *People*: 30-200+ people plus 3-5 facilitation team members
- *Space*: Large flexible space (community center, outdoor venue) with breakout rooms
- *Materials*: Detailed facilitation guide, visual graphics, recording/documentation tools
- *Skills*: Large group facilitation, design thinking, conflict resolution, systems mapping
- *Energy Level*: High—requires careful choreography and sustained focus from facilitators
