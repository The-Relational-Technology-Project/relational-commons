# Living Room Conversations: 150+ Structured Dialogue Guides

> A collection of 150+ carefully designed conversation guides covering topics from healthcare to belonging to money to family to criminal justice. Each guide is structured for 4-7 people to have a 90-minute dialogue that fosters genuine listening and understanding across difference

## What It Is

A collection of 150+ carefully designed conversation guides covering topics from healthcare to belonging to money to family to criminal justice. Each guide is structured for 4-7 people to have a 90-minute dialogue that fosters genuine listening and understanding across differences, specifically designed so anyone can facilitate without expert training.

## How It Works

Participants gather in person or online and follow the guide structure: (1) conversation agreements (confidentiality, respectful listening, staying curious); (2) round 1: personal questions that reveal values and lived experience; (3) round 2: exploration of the specific topic with questions designed to go deeper than surface debate; (4) round 3: finding common ground and exploring possibilities; (5) closing reflection. The pacing is intentionally slow—people listen significantly more than they speak. Guides are designed so facilitators don't need expertise, just willingness to follow the structure and hold space.

## Who 'S Done It

Living Room Conversations have been held in thousands of homes, libraries, and community spaces across the US and internationally. One example: faith communities in rural/urban divides have used the 'Faith' guide to have genuine conversations about religious practice and politics, discovering shared values about community care that transcend surface differences.

## Connections

Emerged from dialogue and deliberation movement; aligns with microsolidarity values of slow, intentional conversation; complements Braver Angels and More in Common approaches.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 90 minutes for conversation, 30 minutes prep for facilitator
- *People*: 4-7 participants with diverse perspectives, 1 facilitator (can be untrained)
- *Space*: Intimate space (living room, small meeting room) for in-person; video call for online
- *Materials*: Conversation guide (free download), optional snacks/beverages
- *Skills*: Basic facilitation; guides are designed for non-experts
- *Energy Level*: Moderate—focused listening rather than debate energy
