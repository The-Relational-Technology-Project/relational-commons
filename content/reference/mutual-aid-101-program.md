# Mutual Aid 101 Program

> Shareable's introductory training program for mutual aid organizing. The program includes live learning sessions with experienced mutual aid practitioners and covers foundational principles and practices for starting and sustaining mutual aid networks.

## What It Is

Shareable's introductory training program for mutual aid organizing. The program includes live learning sessions with experienced mutual aid practitioners and covers foundational principles and practices for starting and sustaining mutual aid networks.

## Connections

Mutual Aid 101 connects to broader work on solidarity economics, community care, and disaster resilience. Shareable's toolkit complements other community organizing resources and skills-sharing practices.
