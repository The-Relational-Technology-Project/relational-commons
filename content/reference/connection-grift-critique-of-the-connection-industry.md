A critical examination of four types of opportunistic exploitation in the civic renewal space: expert grift (monetizing connection advice), technological grift (selling loneliness-solving platforms), political grift (positioning politicians as civic saviors), and civic grift (national nonprofits replicating top-down structures).

Directly critiques approaches in expert consulting, tech platforms, and national nonprofit models; informs decisions about genuine vs. extractive community support.
