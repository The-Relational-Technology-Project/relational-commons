## What It Is

Traditional and contemporary practices of welcoming newcomers to neighborhoods through intentional gestures, information-sharing, and relationship-building. From historic door-to-door welcome wagon visits bearing gifts and community information to modern neighboring practices, welcoming practices lower barriers to community belonging and integration for new residents.

## How It Works

Traditional practice: community members or Chamber of Commerce representatives visit new households with welcome baskets containing local business coupons, service information, church listings, and community resources. Modern practice: neighbors leave welcome notes, deliver baked goods or flower bouquets, offer helpful information (garbage pickup schedules, restaurant recommendations, community maps), provide gifts cards to local businesses, and assist with moving. Some neighborhoods coordinate group welcomes when moving trucks arrive. The goal is immediate connection and belonging.

## Who 'S Done It

Welcome Wagon was widespread in mid-20th century America. Many communities continue welcome traditions through neighborhood associations, chambers of commerce, welcome committees, and individual neighbors. Churches and faith communities often lead formal welcoming practices.

## Connections

Historical practice rooted in small-town neighboring traditions before car-dependent suburbs and isolation. Connected to hospitality practices, block stewards, neighbor-to-neighbor initiatives, and front porch culture. Represents intentional reciprocal neighboring and community integration.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 30 minutes to 1 hour per visit or gesture; minimal ongoing commitment
- *People*: Welcoming neighbor(s), new residents, optional welcome committee coordinator
- *Space*: New resident's home/doorstep, community information gathering place
- *Materials*: Welcome baskets/cards, local restaurant menus, community information sheets, gift items
- *Skills*: Hospitality, community knowledge, clear communication, relationship building
- *Energy Level*: Low—brief, warm, welcoming gestures
