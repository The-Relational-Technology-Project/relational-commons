# Sunday Assembly - Start Your Own Chapter

> A structured guide and support system for launching a new Sunday Assembly chapter in your community. Provides frameworks, mentorship, and resources to help organizers progress from attending existing assemblies to building independent, thriving chapters.

## What It Is

A structured guide and support system for launching a new Sunday Assembly chapter in your community. Provides frameworks, mentorship, and resources to help organizers progress from attending existing assemblies to building independent, thriving chapters.

## How It Works

The startup process follows a four-level progression framework. Level 0 ('Get Engaged') involves attending existing Sunday Assembly events in person or via livestream to understand the community culture. Level 1 ('Assemble') means holding your first gathering—any size gathering in the spirit of Sunday Assembly with a secular perspective and inclusive philosophy. Level 2 ('Get Bigger') involves securing a permanent public venue, marketing through Meetup and Facebook, and building a volunteer team to support operations. Level 3 ('Diversify') launches interest-based subgroups around service activities, hobbies, food, or other shared interests to deepen member engagement. Level 4 ('Become an Organization') formalizes the chapter through nonprofit incorporation, establishing a board, opening a bank account, and fundraising. Throughout this journey, organizers access mentorship through a private online forum where experienced chapter leaders provide guidance. The model emphasizes that chapters remain independent, volunteer-run, free to attend, and radically inclusive.

## Who 'S Done It

Dozens of chapters have successfully launched using this startup framework across North America and internationally. Starting organizers report transforming initial small gatherings of 10-15 people into established communities of 100-300+ attendees. Success stories include chapters that progressed from informal coffee meetings to renting dedicated venues and launching multiple interest-based subgroups. Organizers cite the mentorship forum as essential—connecting them with experienced chapter leaders who guide decisions on venue selection, volunteer recruitment, and community growth. New chapters often serve previously underserved communities seeking secular ethical connection, expanding the network into smaller cities.

## Connections

Directly connected to the Sunday Assembly movement (see main entry). The startup guide adapts organizational development principles common to community organizing, civic associations, and grassroots movements. It reflects best practices from nonprofit scaling while maintaining emphasis on grassroots volunteer leadership.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: Varies by level: Level 1 can start with single 1-2 hour event; progression to Level 4 typically takes 1-2 years
- *People*: 1-3 founding organizers to start; growth to 10-20 core volunteers at mature chapters
- *Space*: Initially flexible (parks, homes, coffee shops); later transitions to public venues (100-300+ capacity)
- *Materials*: Startup guide, mentorship forum access, speaker recommendations, music playlists, volunteer tools, marketing templates
- *Skills*: Event planning, community organizing, public speaking, volunteer management, nonprofit basics (at Level 4)
- *Energy Level*: Medium to high; requires sustained commitment and increasing capacity as chapter grows
