## What It Is

An international federation representing 50,000+ families and teachers operating parent-owned cooperative preschools. Model emphasizes parent participation as board members and co-teachers (one day/week), hiring professional teachers, and shared ownership ensuring quality early education at cooperative principles.

## Connections

Implements cooperative economics and participatory governance in early childhood. Aligns with parent engagement in IAF and Faith in Action models.
