## What It Is

Access intimacy is the sense of ease and safety disabled people experience when someone 'gets' their access needs without judgment or resentment. This form of connection enables authentic presence, reduces the emotional burden of explaining needs, and allows disabled people to exist as whole selves in relationship.

## Connections

Part of disability justice movement and expanding conversations about interdependence. Relates to access work, community care, and intimate justice frameworks. Connects to practices of mutual aid and transformative relationships.
