# Interfaith America BRIDGE Curriculum: Building Regular Interfaith Dialogue

## What It Is

A seven-module training curriculum designed for college and university professionals to facilitate interfaith engagement on campus. Uses a "train the trainer" approach, with modules focusing on worldview identity, bias interrogation, and skills to discuss religious/spiritual identity from multiple perspectives.

## Connections

Complements URI's interfaith dialogue tips. Complements Parish Collective and Nuns & Nones interspiritual community approaches.
