# AARP Experience Corps: Intergenerational Volunteer Tutoring

> An intergenerational volunteer program (founded 1996, joined AARP Foundation 2015) that engages adults 50+ as literacy tutors in public schools. Volunteers provide 6-15 hours weekly supporting struggling readers in one-to-one, small group, or classroom literacy assistance models.

## What It Is

An intergenerational volunteer program (founded 1996, joined AARP Foundation 2015) that engages adults 50+ as literacy tutors in public schools. Volunteers provide 6-15 hours weekly supporting struggling readers in one-to-one, small group, or classroom literacy assistance models.

## Connections

Implements Generations United's intergenerational shared sites philosophy. Complements Encore.org's purposeful aging framework.
