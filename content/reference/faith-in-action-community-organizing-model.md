## What It Is

A faith-based organizing model (founded 1972 as PICO, renamed Faith in Action 2018) where congregations of all denominations serve as institutional bases for community organizations. Uses values-based relationships, community listening, public accountability, and bipartisan engagement to win policy changes.

## Connections

Evolved from IAF's relational organizing model. Sister to other faith-based networks like Parish Collective. Uses power mapping and public accountability practices.
