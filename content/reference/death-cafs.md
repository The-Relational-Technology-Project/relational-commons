## What It Is

Informal community gatherings where people of all ages, beliefs, and backgrounds meet to discuss death, dying, and mortality over tea, coffee, and cake. Death Cafés are agenda-free, non-religious, and focus on increasing awareness of death to help people make the most of their finite lives.

## How It Works

A facilitator gathers a small group (typically 5-20 people) in a comfortable setting with refreshments. The facilitator creates a safe, non-judgmental space for open conversation about death, dying, end-of-life wishes, grief, funeral planning, and what gives life meaning. Discussions are guided by the facilitator but follow the group's natural curiosity and concerns. There is no agenda, advertising, set conclusions, therapy, or counseling—just honest conversation. The environment emphasizes relaxation, community, and shared reflection on a topic most people avoid discussing.

## Who 'S Done It

Over 23,000 Death Cafés held in 97 countries. Started in Switzerland by Bernard Crettaz (2004) and brought to the UK by Jon Underwood (2011). Now held regularly in communities across North America, Europe, Asia, and beyond. Hospitals, hospices, libraries, churches, and community centers host them.

## Connections

Rooted in Swiss and European traditions of death discussion (café mortel). Aligned with death positivity and end-of-life care movements; connected to grief work, narrative medicine, and community healing practices. Related to storytelling circles and talking circles.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 2-3 hours for a session; minimum 1 month for planning and outreach
- *People*: 1 trained facilitator; 5-20 participants
- *Space*: Comfortable, quiet setting: coffee shop, library, church, community center, or private home
- *Materials*: Tea, coffee, cake or light refreshments; comfortable seating; optional: printed discussion prompts or reflection materials
- *Skills*: Facilitation, active listening, creating psychological safety, comfort with silence and emotion
- *Energy Level*: Low to medium—contemplative but emotionally engaged
