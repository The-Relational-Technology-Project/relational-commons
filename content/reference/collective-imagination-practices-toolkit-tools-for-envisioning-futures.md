# Collective Imagination Practices Toolkit: Tools for Envisioning Futures

> An accessible collection of 16 practices, frameworks, meditations, and workshops for individuals and groups to expand imaginative capacity and envision different futures. Covers social, civic, somatic, temporal, and ecological imagination across six thematic sections.

## What It Is

An accessible collection of 16 practices, frameworks, meditations, and workshops for individuals and groups to expand imaginative capacity and envision different futures. Covers social, civic, somatic, temporal, and ecological imagination across six thematic sections.

## Connections

Complements Emergent Strategy's vision-building. Shares roots with Maine Visioning Handbook and Participation Tools for Community Planning.
