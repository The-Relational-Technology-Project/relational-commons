## What It Is

A British Columbia nonprofit (founded 2012) strengthening community resilience through "Connect & Prepare" workshops. Brings neighbors together for three sessions on how social connections build preparedness for acute emergencies and chronic stresses, helping groups identify vulnerabilities and collaboratively build shared resilience.

## Connections

Operationalizes Neighbor-to-Neighbor and WHO Age-Friendly approaches in Canadian context. Complements CERT with relationship-based model.
