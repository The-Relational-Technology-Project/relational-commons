## What It Is

An 18-week intensive training program specifically designed for Black, Indigenous, and People of Color grassroots organizers. Led by women of color trainers, it provides sustained support in relational organizing skills through lectures, small group practice, and ongoing one-on-one coaching.

## Connections

Complements Training for Change and Midwest Academy with specific focus on BIPOC leadership. Builds on IAF and Faith in Action relational organizing foundations.
