## What It Is

A comprehensive resource collection from the Public Library Association providing evidence-based tools, training, and guidance for libraries implementing civic engagement and community-centered programming. Includes facilitation guides, research materials, webinars, and real-world case studies.

## Connections

Part of the broader Libraries Transforming Communities ecosystem. Complements ALA's Libraries Transform initiative and connects with models of libraries as civic infrastructure. References research on libraries' role in shaping community decisions and advancing shared goals.
