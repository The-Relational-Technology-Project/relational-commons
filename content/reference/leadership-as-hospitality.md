# Leadership as Hospitality

> An essay reframing leadership away from hero narratives and heroic authority toward hospitality—creating conditions where others can belong, contribute, and flourish. This approach is foundational to microsolidarity and relational organizing.

## What It Is

An essay reframing leadership away from hero narratives and heroic authority toward hospitality—creating conditions where others can belong, contribute, and flourish. This approach is foundational to microsolidarity and relational organizing.
