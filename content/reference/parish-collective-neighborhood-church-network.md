## What It Is

An ecumenical Christ-following network of practitioners and neighborhood leaders reimagining what it means to be the Church in local neighborhoods. Operates learning communities, conferences, and the Grounded Faith initiative across five US city regions, focused on practicing presence, celebrating place, and listening for God's dreams.

## Connections

Shares values with Faith in Action's congregation-based organizing. Complements Nuns & Nones interspiritual vision with explicitly Christian focus.
