## What It Is

An online community and archive dedicated to creating, sharing, and amplifying disability media and culture. Founded by Alice Wong, the DVP believes disability narratives are important and belong to disabled people themselves. The project includes oral histories, essays, podcasts, anthologies, and campaigns centering disabled voices.

## Connections

Part of disability justice movements and disability culture work. Relates to archival practices, community storytelling, and media activism. Connects to broader movements centering marginalized people's self-representation.
