# How to Reinvent the Potluck

> Shareable's guide transforming potlucks from casual social meals into intentional community organizing tools. By adding structure, facilitation, and shared purpose, potlucks become vehicles for collective decision-making, relationship-building, and community action.

## What It Is

Shareable's guide transforming potlucks from casual social meals into intentional community organizing tools. By adding structure, facilitation, and shared purpose, potlucks become vehicles for collective decision-making, relationship-building, and community action.

## How It Works

Reimagine the potluck as a structured gathering rather than an informal meal. Add facilitation elements such as opening circles for relationship-building, structured conversation prompts or discussion topics relevant to community concerns, group decision-making processes around a shared issue or project, or skill-sharing components. Use the shared meal as a foundation for deeper engagement, moving beyond casual socializing to intentional community-building work. Consider online coordination tools like Potluck, Perfect Potluck, or Meal Train to manage RSVPs, assign dishes, track dietary needs, and share photos/stories from the gathering. Build community through the act of collective contribution and the ritual of breaking bread together.

## Who 'S Done It

Community organizers have used potlucks as organizing tools for decades. Contemporary examples include using potlucks to launch neighborhood organizing campaigns, coordinate mutual aid efforts, build cross-cultural understanding, and strengthen community bonds around shared challenges or values.

## Connections

Potlucks connect to long traditions of communal meals across cultures. This guide reimagines the potluck through an organizing lens, building on traditions while adding intentionality and structure. It relates to community organizing, collective decision-making, and food-based placemaking.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 2-3 hours for event; 2-3 weeks planning
- *People*: Organizers; diverse community members; facilitation team
- *Space*: Flexible (home, park, community center)
- *Materials*: Food dishes; online coordination tools; facilitation guides; conversation prompts
- *Skills*: Community facilitation, group dynamics management, basic organizing
- *Energy Level*: Moderate; builds community while accomplishing organizing work
