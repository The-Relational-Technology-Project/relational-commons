## What It Is

A community-building model developed by Sam Pressler through the Armed Services Arts Partnership that identifies five essential elements for sustained member engagement: Compelling activities, Comprehensive pathways, Commitment through reciprocity, Consistent scheduling, and Continuous advancement opportunities.

## Connections

Informs practice-based community programs that need retention strategies, complements frameworks about participation depth and member development.
