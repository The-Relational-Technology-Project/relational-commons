# URI Interfaith Dialogue Tips: Designing and Facilitating Dialogue

> Practical guidance from United Religions Initiative on designing and facilitating interfaith dialogue in communities. Emphasizes creating safe space, establishing ground rules, modeling dialogue qualities, learning-oriented approach, and facilitator preparation.

## What It Is

Practical guidance from United Religions Initiative on designing and facilitating interfaith dialogue in communities. Emphasizes creating safe space, establishing ground rules, modeling dialogue qualities, learning-oriented approach, and facilitator preparation.

## Connections

Complements Interfaith America BRIDGE curriculum with practical facilitation skills. Supports Parish Collective and Nuns & Nones community-building work.
