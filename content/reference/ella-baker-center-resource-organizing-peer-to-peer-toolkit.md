# Ella Baker Center Resource Organizing: Peer-to-Peer Toolkit

> A peer-to-peer resource organizing toolkit and curriculum from the Ella Baker Center for Human Rights designed to help people engage friends and family in campaigns, events, and movements. Shows how to mobilize people's talents, ideas, and networks using Peer-to-Peer strategy.

## What It Is

A peer-to-peer resource organizing toolkit and curriculum from the Ella Baker Center for Human Rights designed to help people engage friends and family in campaigns, events, and movements. Shows how to mobilize people's talents, ideas, and networks using Peer-to-Peer strategy.

## Connections

Complements IAF's relational organizing and Faith in Action's values-based mobilization. Part of broader peer-to-peer organizing tradition.
