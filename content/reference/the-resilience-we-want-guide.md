## What It Is

Shareable's 12-page guide for transforming community spaces into resilience hubs where neighbors build belonging, learn together, meet basic needs, prepare for disasters, and strengthen mutual aid. The guide applies to diverse spaces including libraries, schools, social clubs, cafes, religious centers, and artist studios.

## Connections

The Resilience We Want connects to the Library of Things, Mutual Aid 101, and community space activation. It aligns with disaster resilience, community care, and commons-based organizing movements.
