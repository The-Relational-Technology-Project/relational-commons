# DoSomething.org

> A digital platform and community hub empowering young people (ages 13-25) to become civic leaders and activists. With over 1 million active members globally, DoSomething.org provides youth with tools, campaigns, and peer community to take action on issues they care about.

## What It Is

A digital platform and community hub empowering young people (ages 13-25) to become civic leaders and activists. With over 1 million active members globally, DoSomething.org provides youth with tools, campaigns, and peer community to take action on issues they care about.

## How It Works

Young people engage through multiple pathways: (1) Browse and select causes—climate action, mental health, book banning, food insecurity, and many others. (2) Participate in specific campaigns with concrete action steps—organizing street cleanups, collecting period products for unhoused individuals, creating educational PSAs. (3) Earn volunteer credits toward scholarships and recognition. (4) Connect with other changemakers across the U.S. and 189 countries. The platform emphasizes accessibility and achievable action, breaking down large issues into doable campaigns. Youth develop leadership skills through participation, build peer networks around shared causes, and experience tangible impact from collective action.

## Who 'S Done It

DoSomething.org has mobilized over 8 million young people across U.S. area codes and internationally. Campaigns demonstrate diverse youth leadership—from environmental activism to social justice organizing. Young people report building confidence, developing skills, and finding 'their people' around shared values. The platform has created measurable social impact through coordinated youth campaigns addressing systemic issues.

## Connections

Part of youth organizing and civic engagement movements. Relates to broader digital activism and social change platforms. Connects to youth leadership development, peer organizing, and accessible activism frameworks.
