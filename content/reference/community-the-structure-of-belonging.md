## What It Is

Peter Block's 2008 book that reframes community building around 'belonging'—the experience of being part of something, having voice and agency. Block argues authentic community emerges not from problem-solving but from conversations that build relationships, possibility, and shared ownership.

## How It Works

Block proposes five types of conversations that create the conditions for belonging: (1) conversations of possibility—what could be, what we want to build together; (2) conversations of ownership—how do we take responsibility, what's ours to do; (3) conversations of dissent—creating space for real disagreement and diverse views; (4) conversations of commitment—how will we show up, what are we promising; (5) conversations of gifts—recognizing and building on each other's capacities. The book emphasizes that transformation is linguistic and relational—real change happens when communities shift how they speak and listen together.

## Who 'S Done It

Block's frameworks have influenced community development practitioners across North America. Documented example: A neighborhood struggling with disinvestment applied Block's conversation model, moving from meetings focused on 'what's wrong' to structured dialogues about assets, possibility, and shared commitment. Within two years, residents had initiated a community garden, started a tool library, and strengthened existing informal networks.

## Connections

Builds on traditions of participatory community development, appreciative inquiry, and relational sociology; directly influences microsolidarity frameworks and Living Room Conversations design.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: Months to years for deep community shift; individual conversations 1-2 hours
- *People*: Start with 5-10 core people, build to 50-100+ in community
- *Space*: Multiple gathering spaces in neighborhood (homes, cafes, parks, community centers)
- *Materials*: Conversation guides, facilitation training resources, documentation tools
- *Skills*: Facilitation, systems thinking, appreciative listening, group convening
- *Energy Level*: Sustained moderate—ongoing patience and relationship-tending
