## What It Is

Dean Spade's foundational text on mutual aid as a grassroots theory and organizing practice. Published by Verso in 2020, this 128-page guide addresses why mutual aid matters, what it looks like in practice, and provides concrete tools for organizing groups around meeting community needs while pursuing transformative social change.

## Connections

Directly informs the Big Door Brigade Toolbox and microsolidarity frameworks; complements both practice-based and theoretical approaches to community building.
