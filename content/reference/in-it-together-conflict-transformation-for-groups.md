## What It Is

A conflict resolution toolkit developed through the Interrupting Criminalization project, designed specifically for activist groups and mutual aid organizations. This resource provides frameworks and practical tools for transforming conflict within communities rather than relying on external authority figures or abandoning relationships.

## Connections

Complements Dean Spade's capacity-building work and microsolidarity frameworks; aligned with restorative justice and transformative justice movements.
