## What It Is

A framework and practice for using storytelling strategically to shift cultural narratives, build power, and drive social change. Recognizing that narratives—collections of stories embodying shared values—shape how people understand problems and possibilities, practitioners use strategic storytelling to challenge dominant narratives, amplify community narratives, and create openings for policy and cultural change.

## Who 'S Done It

Narrative Arts (formerly Working Narratives) leads narrative change practice nationally. Applied by social justice organizations, grassroots movements, union campaigns, immigrant rights groups, environmental justice organizations, and community storytelling projects. Increasingly mainstream in advocacy and organizing work.

## Connections

Developed through Working Narratives' practice starting in early 2000s. Connected to public narrative (Ganz), beautiful trouble, and creative activism. Related to story-sharing practices, StoryCorps, and community lore traditions. Part of broader understanding of narrative power in social movements.
