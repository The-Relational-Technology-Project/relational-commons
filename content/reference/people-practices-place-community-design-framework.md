## What It Is

A framework for understanding how communities form and thrive through the interaction of three elements: the people (who they are, their relationships), the practices (what they do together), and the place (physical and digital spaces). This essay synthesizes microsolidarity theory into a practical framework for community builders.

## Connections

Complements Peter Block's work on conversations and belonging; informs how to design comprehensive community-building initiatives.
