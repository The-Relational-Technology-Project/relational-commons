# Power Mapping and Analysis: Strategic Campaign Planning

> A strategic planning tool from the Commons Library that helps campaigns understand the decision-making landscape and identify leverage points. Groups map who holds power to make decisions, who influences them, and what relationships and avenues exist for reaching decision-makers.

## What It Is

A strategic planning tool from the Commons Library that helps campaigns understand the decision-making landscape and identify leverage points. Groups map who holds power to make decisions, who influences them, and what relationships and avenues exist for reaching decision-makers.

## Connections

Core tool used alongside Faith in Action, IAF, and Training for Change organizing. Complements Emergent Strategy's fractal thinking with concrete power analysis.
