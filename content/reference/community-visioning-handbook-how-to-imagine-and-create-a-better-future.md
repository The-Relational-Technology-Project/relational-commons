# Community Visioning Handbook: How to Imagine and Create a Better Future

> A step-by-step guide from Maine's Department of Agriculture for communities to develop shared visions before comprehensive planning. The handbook distinguishes vision (the dream of what community should be/feel like) from planning (the blueprint of how to get there).

## What It Is

A step-by-step guide from Maine's Department of Agriculture for communities to develop shared visions before comprehensive planning. The handbook distinguishes vision (the dream of what community should be/feel like) from planning (the blueprint of how to get there).

## Connections

Practical implementation of Collective Imagination Practices. Used alongside CivicWell participation tools for comprehensive community planning.
