## What It Is

A proven poverty-reduction model that builds intentional relationships across income lines, expanding the social capital of people living in poverty. Circles is grounded in research showing that low-income individuals need connections across class and cultural lines to improve economic situations—those with strong diverse networks have greater access to opportunity, jobs, and resources.

## How It Works

Circle Leaders (people living in or near poverty) are matched with Allies (volunteers from middle/upper-income backgrounds). Matched pairs meet weekly for minimum 18 months. The Ally provides mentorship, connects the Circle Leader to their own professional and social networks, helps develop skills, and provides accountability without judgment. Weekly group meetings support all participants. After 18 months, participants see 71% average income increase and expanded personal networks. The model recognizes that friendship across class lines fundamentally shifts opportunity and mindsets.

## Who 'S Done It

Circles USA operates in multiple cities nationwide including Indianapolis, Baltimore, Denver, Northwest Arkansas, and others. The model has demonstrated poverty reduction outcomes and is being scaled nationally with evaluation research supporting its effectiveness.

## Connections

Developed as alternative to traditional poverty programs that focus on deficits and barriers. Connected to high-agency thinking, social capital theory, relational organizing, and abundance frameworks. Related to coaching models and mentoring but distinguished by emphasis on friendship and mutual benefit rather than expert-to-client relationships.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 18+ months minimum commitment; weekly meetings 1-2 hours each
- *People*: Circle Leaders, Allies, Group Facilitators, program coordinators, diverse socioeconomic mix
- *Space*: Community locations for weekly meetings and group gatherings
- *Materials*: Curriculum guides, communication tools, network maps, goal-tracking resources
- *Skills*: Relationship building, active listening, cross-class communication, goal facilitation
- *Energy Level*: Moderate to high—regular meetings, relationship work, intentional bridge-building
