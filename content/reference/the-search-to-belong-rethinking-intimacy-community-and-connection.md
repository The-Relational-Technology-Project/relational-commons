## What It Is

Joseph Myers' examination of how humans experience belonging through four types of spatial relationships: public (crowds, large gatherings), social (acquaintances, networks), personal (friends, deeper connection), and intimate (family, close partners). Myers argues that healthy communities need all four scales of relationship, and that most community-building efforts neglect the relational dynamics that make belonging possible.

## Connections

Directly informs microsolidarity's attention to crews (personal) scaling into congregations (social/public); complements Peter Block's work on conversation types and scales; informs Living Room Conversations design for personal-scale dialogue.
