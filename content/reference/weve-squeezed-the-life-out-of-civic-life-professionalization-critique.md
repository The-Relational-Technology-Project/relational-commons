# We've Squeezed the Life Out of Civic Life: Professionalization Critique

> An examination of how professionalization and credentialization have damaged civic participation by erecting barriers that exclude ordinary citizens, replacing broad community membership with narrow expert management and transactional service delivery.

## What It Is
