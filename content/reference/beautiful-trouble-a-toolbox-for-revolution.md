## What It Is

A collection of over 300 stories, tactics, principles, theories and methodologies for creative activism written by 175+ frontline activists worldwide. The toolbox functions as a pattern language for designing campaigns and actions, with resources available as a book, online platform, strategy cards, and training programs.

## Connections

Complements Emergent Strategy's philosophy with concrete tactical tools. Shares DNA with Training for Change and Community Tool Box approaches to participatory learning.
