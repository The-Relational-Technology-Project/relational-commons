## What It Is

A foundational framework articulating 10 core principles of disability justice as developed by the disability justice movement, exemplified through the work of Sins Invalid (a disability justice performance project). The principles guide collective organizing, community care, and justice work centered on disabled people's leadership and self-determination.

## Connections

Relates to broader disability rights, accessibility movements, and liberation frameworks. Connects to queer justice, racial justice, and trans liberation movements. Part of evolving conversations about access intimacy, interdependence, and transformative justice.
