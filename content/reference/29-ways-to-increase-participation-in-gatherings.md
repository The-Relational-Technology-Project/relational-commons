## What It Is

A facilitation toolkit of 29 specific tactics for moving gatherings from one-person-speaking-to-many toward many-people-speaking-with-each-other. These practices cover warm-ups, interactive presentations, and group decision-making — creating conditions where quieter people feel safe participating.

## How It Works

Tactics organized into three phases. In Openings: use collaborative agenda-setting, rotate who makes announcements, do group warmups with movement, check the pulse of how people feel. Key principle: everyone must talk and laugh in the first 10 minutes. In Main Shares: embed 'raise your hand if...' moments, rotate presenters, offer unconference-style sessions, use '1-2-4-All' (individual reflection → pair → small group → whole group) so people build comfort before speaking publicly. For Decision-making: warm up with low-stakes prompts first, practice new methods before using them for important decisions, provide extensive choice in how people participate (some prefer thinking quietly, others need to move). The underlying philosophy: move from 'Hub' structures (power centered on one person) toward 'Web' structures with distributed agency.

## Who 'S Done It

Oakland used these tactics in a participatory budgeting process, shifting from public hearings (where mostly powerful people speak) to participatory design. They opened with group warmups, used small-group conversations, applied 1-2-4-All for brainstorming, and rotated facilitators. Participation more than tripled. A quieter community member said: 'I never would have spoken up, but starting with a pair conversation made it feel safe. By the time we talked as a whole group, I had things to say.'

## Connections

These tactics draw on Liberating Structures, participatory democracy traditions, popular education, and feminist meeting facilitation. They connect to World Café, Civic Saturdays, and circle processes, offering specific tools for inclusion. They're rooted in recognition that how we meet shapes what we can decide together. Created by Elise Granata at GROUP HUG.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: Variable — tactics range from 5-minute warmups to full-session design
- *People*: Works at any group size from 5 to 200+
- *Space*: Flexible — works in-person and online
- *Materials*: Depends on tactic (timers, notecards, flip charts, shared documents)
- *Skills*: Comfort with facilitation, understanding of power dynamics, creativity
- *Energy Level*: Moderate to high — requires active facilitation
