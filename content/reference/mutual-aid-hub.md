# Mutual Aid Hub

> A directory and network coordination platform that helps connect mutual aid projects and organizers across regions. The Hub serves as a discovery tool for people starting or joining mutual aid work, and as a coordination infrastructure for multi-project solidarity networks.

## What It Is

A directory and network coordination platform that helps connect mutual aid projects and organizers across regions. The Hub serves as a discovery tool for people starting or joining mutual aid work, and as a coordination infrastructure for multi-project solidarity networks.

## Connections

Complements Big Door Brigade's toolkit approach; supports the scaling logic described in microsolidarity theory about how crews connect into larger networks.
