## What It Is

A research and community activation initiative exploring gaming's role in urban development and collaborative city-making. Games for Cities creates platforms, knowledge resources, and networks connecting game designers, urban planners, and community members to use games for improving urban design processes, collaborative decision-making, and community engagement.

## Connections

Relates to broader civic games movements and participatory urban planning frameworks. Connects play theory to urban governance and community development. Part of emerging field exploring games for social change and collaborative decision-making in cities.
