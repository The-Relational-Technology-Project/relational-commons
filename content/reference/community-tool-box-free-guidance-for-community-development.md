# Community Tool Box: Free Guidance for Community Development

> A free, comprehensive online resource from the University of Kansas containing 7,000+ pages of practical step-by-step guidance across 46 chapters on community-building skills. Provides a simple model for assessing, planning, acting, evaluating, and sustaining community work.

## What It Is

A free, comprehensive online resource from the University of Kansas containing 7,000+ pages of practical step-by-step guidance across 46 chapters on community-building skills. Provides a simple model for assessing, planning, acting, evaluating, and sustaining community work.

## Connections

Serves as foundational resource for organizing efforts described in Training for Change and other platforms. Complements Beautiful Trouble with systematic frameworks.
