# How Our Online Lives Shape Our Community

> An analysis of how social media and digital platforms create feedback loops that damage in-person community engagement, particularly affecting isolated individuals who turn to online spaces that promote isolation rather than genuine belonging.

## What It Is

An analysis of how social media and digital platforms create feedback loops that damage in-person community engagement, particularly affecting isolated individuals who turn to online spaces that promote isolation rather than genuine belonging.

## Connections

Informs digital strategy decisions for community organizing and complements work on relational, place-based technology alternatives.
