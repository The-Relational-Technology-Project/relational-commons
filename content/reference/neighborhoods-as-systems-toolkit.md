## What It Is

A comprehensive guide and collection of 26 tools for community-led systems change at the neighborhood level. Co-created by Cincinnati Children's Hospital Medical Center with residents and community partners, the toolkit helps neighborhoods identify challenges, envision solutions, test interventions, and measure impact through a structured four-phase approach.

## Connections

The toolkit complements placemaking, participatory budgeting, and asset-based community development approaches. It provides structured methodology for community organizing and serves as framework for deploying facilitation practices like Appreciative Inquiry and participatory planning.
