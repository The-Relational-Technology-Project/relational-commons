## What It Is

An interspiritual alliance (founded 2016) bringing together Catholic sisters, spiritually diverse millennials, and key partners to explore new forms of community life centered on repair, renewal, and responding to urgent calls of the time. No requirement for religious belief to participate.

## Connections

Complements Interfaith America and Parish Collective approaches. Exemplifies faith-rooted community building across generations and traditions.
