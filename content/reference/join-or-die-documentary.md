# Join or Die Documentary

> Emmy-nominated feature documentary examining the decline and potential revival of civic engagement in America. The film follows legendary social scientist Robert Putnam's research on social capital and highlights examples of communities successfully rebuilding civic participation

## What It Is

Emmy-nominated feature documentary examining the decline and potential revival of civic engagement in America. The film follows legendary social scientist Robert Putnam's research on social capital and highlights examples of communities successfully rebuilding civic participation and belonging.

## Connections

Join or Die directly builds on Robert Putnam's social capital research and complements works like Priya Parker's on 'The Art of Gathering.' It relates to broader conversations about rebuilding democracy and community in America.
