## What It Is

A measurement framework developed by the National Conference on Citizenship that assesses community wellbeing through indicators of civic engagement: trust, participation, volunteering, voting, community organizing, and institutional connection. It provides data-driven insights into civic health across cities, states, and nationally.

## Connections

Civic Health Index data complements community development efforts, informing priorities for placemaking, organizing, and civic infrastructure investment. It provides baseline metrics useful for evaluating programs like timebanks, neighborhood grants, and community building initiatives. It relates conceptually to asset-based community development and social capital measurement.
