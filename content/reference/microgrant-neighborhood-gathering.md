## What It Is

A simple funding mechanism that gives neighbors $50-100 to host their own community gatherings, removing the barrier of cost and democratizing who can convene people. Paired with organizing support — checklists, facilitation guides — it trusts that neighbors know what their communities need.

## How It Works

The process is intentionally frictionless. Interested hosts complete a 3-5 minute application describing their gathering and its connection to neighborhood life. Applications are reviewed and approved within one business day. The host receives their $50-100 grant plus an organizing toolkit with facilitation checklists, sample agendas, communication templates. They host their gathering — block party, potluck, seasonal celebration, community conversation. After the event, the only requirement is a photo and brief notes on what happened and any commitments that emerged. No receipts needed, no complex reporting. The whole cycle emphasizes simplicity and trust.

## Who 'S Done It

In Connective Tissue's Charlottesville pilot in spring 2023, they received 70 applications and distributed $100 microgrants to 30 hosts, reaching nearly 500 neighbors across 25 neighborhoods in a few weeks. Almost every attendee committed to concrete next steps afterward — some started recurring monthly gatherings, others organized neighborhood improvements, many deepened relationships with people they'd never met. One host noted the barrier wasn't desire or ideas — it was the psychological lift of asking 'Can I do this?' The $100 answered that question.

## Connections

Builds on ABCD wisdom that people closest to a problem are closest to the solution. Connected to participatory budgeting and subsidiarity — decisions and resources flowing to the most local level. Connective Tissue is part of a movement recognizing that civic infrastructure has eroded and small, repeatable gestures rebuild it. Pairs with Front Porch Forum for amplification and Public Narrative for articulation.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 3-5 min to apply, 2-4 hours to host, 10 min to document
- *People*: 1 host + 8-15 neighbors; coordinating team for applications
- *Space*: Any welcoming neighborhood space (porch, backyard, park, community room)
- *Materials*: Basic supplies for the gathering type
- *Skills*: Basic facilitation, ability to reach neighbors
- *Energy Level*: Moderate
