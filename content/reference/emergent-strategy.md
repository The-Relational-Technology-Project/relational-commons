## What It Is

A philosophy and framework for movement-building and social transformation developed by adrienne maree brown, drawing on complexity science, natural systems, Octavia Butler's work, and 25 years of movement facilitation. It offers principles for collective leadership, adaptation, and decentralized power-building grounded in resilience, interdependence, and transformative justice.

## Connections

Relates to transformative justice frameworks, pleasure activism, radical imagination practices, and decentralized organizing models used by IAF and other community networks.
