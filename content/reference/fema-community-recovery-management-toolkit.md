## What It Is

A comprehensive compilation of guidance, case studies, tools, and training from FEMA to help communities manage long-term disaster recovery. Covers organizing/leadership, needs assessment/planning, identifying programs/resources, and managing grants/finances through three-step recovery process.

## Connections

Complements Mutual Aid Disaster Relief solidarity approach with systems frameworks. Pairs with Building Resilient Neighbourhoods and CERT preparation.
