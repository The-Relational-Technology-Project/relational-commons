## What It Is

A comprehensive research study by More in Common (2018) that surveyed 8,000 Americans using advanced statistical clustering to identify seven distinct 'tribes' with different values, beliefs, and worldviews. A key finding: the Exhausted Majority—two-thirds of Americans—are tired of polarization and hungry for common ground, though their voice is often absent from political discourse.

## Connections

Informs polarization reduction work including Living Room Conversations, Braver Angels, and Weave initiatives; provides empirical foundation for bridging and connection-building practices.
