# Weave Learning Center: Training for Community Weavers

> An online learning platform providing training and resources for community weavers. The center offers courses, toolkits, and peer learning spaces for people working to strengthen social connection and community cohesion at the local level.

## What It Is

An online learning platform providing training and resources for community weavers. The center offers courses, toolkits, and peer learning spaces for people working to strengthen social connection and community cohesion at the local level.

## Connections

Provides practical training aligned with microsolidarity, Living Room Conversations, and relational organizing frameworks.
