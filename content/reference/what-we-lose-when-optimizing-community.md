# What We Lose When Optimizing Community

> A critique of how treating community building as a professional, polished endeavor—requiring social media presence, graphic design, constant content—makes grassroots organizing harder to initiate and erases the value of excellent volunteer-run work.

## What It Is

A critique of how treating community building as a professional, polished endeavor—requiring social media presence, graphic design, constant content—makes grassroots organizing harder to initiate and erases the value of excellent volunteer-run work.

## Connections

Critiques professionalization and supports low-barrier, grassroots approaches to community building.
