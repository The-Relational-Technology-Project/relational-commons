# Bowling Alone: The Collapse and Revival of American Community

> Robert Putnam's foundational research on social capital decline in the United States. Using nearly 500,000 interviews over 25 years, Putnam documents decreasing civic participation, community involvement, and social trust since the 1970s.

## What It Is

Robert Putnam's foundational research on social capital decline in the United States. Using nearly 500,000 interviews over 25 years, Putnam documents decreasing civic participation, community involvement, and social trust since the 1970s.

## Connections

Bowling Alone provides the empirical foundation for contemporary civic renewal efforts and is directly cited in the Join or Die documentary. The distinction between 'bonding' and 'bridging' social capital has become foundational to community organizing and civic infrastructure work.
