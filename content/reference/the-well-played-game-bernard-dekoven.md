# The Well-Played Game (Bernard DeKoven)

> A foundational philosophy text by Bernard DeKoven exploring play as a path to human flourishing, freedom, and connection. The book articulates how playfulness—characterized by joy, creativity, and mutual delight—can transform personal well-being, relationships, and communities.

## What It Is

A foundational philosophy text by Bernard DeKoven exploring play as a path to human flourishing, freedom, and connection. The book articulates how playfulness—characterized by joy, creativity, and mutual delight—can transform personal well-being, relationships, and communities.

## Connections

Central to the New Games Movement philosophy and contemporary play-based community organizing. Influences modern initiatives like Come Out and Play Festival, A Playful Path, and community games programs. Connects to broader practices of play in education, conflict resolution, and civic engagement.
