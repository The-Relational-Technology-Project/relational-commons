# Grandfamilies & Kinship Support Network

> A network operated by Generations United (with four managing partners) supporting the 2.6 million children in the US being raised by grandparents, other relatives, or close family friends. Provides resources, data, and advocacy to improve supports for these families.

## What It Is

A network operated by Generations United (with four managing partners) supporting the 2.6 million children in the US being raised by grandparents, other relatives, or close family friends. Provides resources, data, and advocacy to improve supports for these families.

## Connections

Part of Generations United's broader intergenerational work. Complements AARP and Encore programs with focus on kinship caregiving.
