# All-America Conversations Toolkit

> A facilitated dialogue process created by the National Civic League for communities to have meaningful conversations across differences, understand shared aspirations, identify divisions, and discover concrete actions that build confidence in working together across dividing line

## What It Is

A facilitated dialogue process created by the National Civic League for communities to have meaningful conversations across differences, understand shared aspirations, identify divisions, and discover concrete actions that build confidence in working together across dividing lines.

## How It Works

Recruit 20-50 community members representing diverse perspectives and backgrounds. Hold 2-3 hour conversation session with trained facilitator. Facilitate discussion around questions: What are your hopes and dreams for our community? What divisions or challenges do we face? What small, specific actions give you confidence we can work together? Create psychological safety through ground rules, confidentiality, and respectful listening. Assign a note-taker documenting key themes and insights. The toolkit provides sample recruitment letters, facilitator tips, ground rules, and media outreach guidance. Conversations often catalyze subsequent organizing or action planning. Communities can request free consulting from the National Civic League to customize conversations to local issues.

## Connections

All-America Conversations draws from dialogue facilitation, deliberative democracy traditions, and civic engagement practice. It shares principles with World Café and Circle processes, adapted specifically for building cross-dividing-line relationships in polarized contexts.
