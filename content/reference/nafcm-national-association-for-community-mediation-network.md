## What It Is

A membership association of peacemakers (established 1995, with roots in 1964 Civil Rights Act) supporting 400+ community mediation centers with 20,000+ volunteer mediators across the US. Provides training institutes, mini-grants, quality assurance, and research on mediation effectiveness.

## Connections

Complements neighbor connection work in Building Resilient Neighbourhoods and Neighbor-to-Neighbor programs. Supports relationship-building in faith-based organizing.
