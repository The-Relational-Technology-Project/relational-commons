# Buy Nothing Group

> A hyperlocal gift economy community where neighbors share possessions, gifts, and services free of charge. Operating on the principle that giving and receiving happen without expectation of payment, Buy Nothing builds interdependence and reduces consumption. 7.5 million+ members 

## What It Is

A hyperlocal gift economy community where neighbors share possessions, gifts, and services free of charge. Operating on the principle that giving and receiving happen without expectation of payment, Buy Nothing builds interdependence and reduces consumption. 7.5 million+ members worldwide.

## How It Works

Search Facebook for 'Buy Nothing + [your neighborhood]'. If none exists, create a new private group for your hyperlocal area — boundaries should be tight so members are true neighbors. Guidelines: no buying, selling, bartering, or trading; all items given without expectation of return. Members post items to give away ('offering'), items they need ('asking'), or services they can provide. Others respond and arrange direct pickup. Admins remove commercial posts and unkind behavior. The culture is abundance, gratitude, and trust. Members develop relationships through repeated giving and receiving.

## Who 'S Done It

In 2013, friends Liesl Clark and Rebecca Rockefeller on Bainbridge Island, Washington noticed plastic waste on local beaches. They experimented with a gift economy Facebook group. The idea resonated deeply — within years, Buy Nothing expanded to 7,500+ groups with 6.5 million members across multiple continents. What began as a response to pollution became a cultural shift in how communities think about ownership, abundance, and interdependence.

## Connections

Emerges from gift economy traditions, Freecycle, and commons stewardship. Connects to time banking, skill-shares, and broader degrowth movements. Challenges capitalist assumptions about property while building social capital and community resilience.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 10-20 minutes daily (flexible engagement)
- *People*: 20-500+ active members per group, 1-2 admins
- *Space*: Online (Facebook or alternative), optional in-person meetups
- *Materials*: None required
- *Skills*: Basic social media fluency, moderation
- *Energy Level*: Low
