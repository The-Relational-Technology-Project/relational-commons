## What It Is

A national community organizing network (founded 1940) that pioneered the modern faith-based and broad-based organizing model. Uses relational organizing—building reciprocal relationships within and between institutions—as the foundation for developing citizen leadership and power.

## Connections

Foundation for Faith in Action model. Complements IAF's approach with power mapping (Commons Library) and emerges as precursor to later faith-organizing networks.
