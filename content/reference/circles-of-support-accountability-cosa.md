## What It Is

A volunteer-driven community practice that provides intentional wraparound support for individuals transitioning back into community life, particularly people with complex needs who require both accountability and genuine human connection. Circles create relationships based on mutuality, equality, and covenant—volunteer community members commit to walking alongside a person through significant life changes.

## How It Works

A core member (person needing support) is matched with 4-6 volunteer supporters who commit to regular meetings (typically weekly) for an extended period (minimum 18 months). The inner circle meets with the core member for social support, skill development, and relationship building. A circle coordinator manages recruitment and organizational logistics. An outer circle of professionals provides specialized support. All operate from a covenant of mutual commitment and shared responsibility, not case management.

## Who 'S Done It

Originally developed in Canadian Aboriginal and First Nations traditions, adapted in Canada and the United States for criminal justice reentry contexts. Inside Circle and CSG Justice Center have implemented CoSA models nationally. Faith communities have adapted the circle model for disability support, family strengthening, and community reentry.

## Connections

Rooted in Indigenous circling traditions and justice practices emphasizing restoration over punishment. Connects to affinity groups, Circles USA poverty-bridging work, community support models, and restorative justice. Related to the principle of access intimacy and mutual aid-based care.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 18+ months commitment per person, ongoing recruitment
- *People*: Core member, 4-6 dedicated volunteers, circle coordinator, professional advisors, community base
- *Space*: Community locations for weekly meetings (homes, faith spaces, community centers)
- *Materials*: Covenant agreements, communication tools, resource referral lists
- *Skills*: Relationship building, active listening, accountability without judgment, boundary setting
- *Energy Level*: High—requires deep personal commitment and emotional availability from volunteers
