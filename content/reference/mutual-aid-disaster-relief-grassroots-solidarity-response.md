## What It Is

A grassroots network responding to disasters through solidarity (not charity), working with affected communities—especially vulnerable members—to lead their own recovery. Provides supplies, medical support, work crews, and amplifies community-led initiatives that emerge after disasters.

## Connections

Complements CERT and FEMA Community Recovery Toolkit with values-based approach. Implements Emergent Strategy principles in disaster response contexts.
