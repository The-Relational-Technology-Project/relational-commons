## What It Is

Shareable's comprehensive 12-section toolkit for starting and growing Libraries of Things (LoTs) in communities. A Library of Things is a community hub where residents borrow tools, equipment, and items rather than buying them individually, similar to traditional libraries but for material goods.

## Connections

LoTs exemplify the sharing economy and solidarity economics, complementing mutual aid, tool libraries, and community resilience initiatives. They relate to resource sharing, anti-consumerism, and commons-based organizing.
