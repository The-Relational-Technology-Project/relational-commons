# Braver Angels Skills Workshops: Bridging Across Polarization

> Skills training workshops offered by Braver Angels, a bipartisan organization dedicated to reducing political polarization. Workshops teach practical skills for navigating disagreement, finding common ground, and building genuine relationships across political divides.

## What It Is

Skills training workshops offered by Braver Angels, a bipartisan organization dedicated to reducing political polarization. Workshops teach practical skills for navigating disagreement, finding common ground, and building genuine relationships across political divides.

## How It Works

Workshops typically include: (1) skills training in active listening, perspective-taking, and finding shared values; (2) practice conversations between people with different political views; (3) reflection on what's possible when people genuinely listen; (4) tools for continuing this work in home communities. Workshops emphasize that common ground is not about splitting differences but about recognizing underlying shared concerns (security, opportunity, belonging) that might manifest in different political positions.

## Who 'S Done It

Thousands of people have attended Braver Angels workshops in red and blue communities across the US. One documented case: a rural workshop in Kentucky brought together farmers, union workers, and business owners who expected fierce disagreement but discovered overlapping concerns about economic stability, community investment, and dignity in work.

## Connections

Part of bridging movement alongside Living Room Conversations and More in Common work; informs larger social cohesion field.

## Key Ingredients

- *Time*: 3-4 hours per workshop, usually half-day or full-day format
- *People*: 10-30 participants with mixed political views, plus trained facilitators
- *Space*: Neutral community space (library, community center, faith space)
- *Materials*: Workshop guides, conversation protocols, reflection tools
- *Skills*: Facilitation, active listening, managing group dynamics
- *Energy Level*: Moderate to high—intellectually and emotionally engaging
