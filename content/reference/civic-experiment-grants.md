# Civic Experiment Grants

> Trust for Civic Life's grant program supporting 100+ pilot projects and civic experiments across rural America. Grants up to $25,000 fund innovative, community-driven initiatives addressing local challenges through civic participation and collective action.

## What It Is

Trust for Civic Life's grant program supporting 100+ pilot projects and civic experiments across rural America. Grants up to $25,000 fund innovative, community-driven initiatives addressing local challenges through civic participation and collective action.

## Connections

The grants operationalize the 'Action Over Dialogue' research by supporting projects that bring people together to solve problems. Grantees often develop into Civic Hub models for ongoing coordination.
