# Hyperlocal Neighbor Hub

> Bring care, resources, and connection to your block

![](https://studio.relationaltechproject.org/images/gallery/hyperlocal_neighbor_hub.png)

A hyperlocal neighbor hub that brings care, resources, and connection to a specific neighborhood or block. The first one formed on a single block in San Francisco after a coffee and donuts gathering, to help the neighbors "support each other and plan our next party."
